## Capabilities and page behavior

- Graphic arrival: substantial authored landscape illustration integrated with
  proposition and acquisition/service Actions. No dashboard or fabricated site.
- Bounded layering: `field-bridge` for a Hero; `artifact-bridge` for an intro,
  collection or invitation. Fixed family-owned overlaps; no offsets, coordinates,
  customer breakpoints or freeform inputs. Layers require resolved preview media.
- Continuing fields: semantic stage text in connected branded regions; supported
  Expanded scope remains attached to Launch. Mobile retains a shared visual plane.
- Shared contributions: party-owned explanations with a shared approval statement;
  scope uses category-based annotations. Relationship contracts remain authoritative.
- Evidence feature: substantial preview-only featured and supporting positions with
  business-context/contribution explanations. No uncleared identities or screenshots.
- Artifact family: fixed labeled report regions, three illustrative priority positions,
  next-path outline and annotation fragments. No actual findings or report-builder UI.
- Graphic sign-off: approved light wordmark, concise service statement, navigation
  and connected graphic edge. Only already verified identity is rendered.

Home follows branded arrival → compact recognition → continuing relationship →
responsibility/credibility → tangible Review invitation. Service emphasizes the
connected relationship before detailed scope/pricing/responsibilities. Experience
reserves image weight despite unresolved rights. Review makes the report the principal
visual alongside practical input/output and scope explanation. Motion remains Off.

## FPO and safety boundary

FPO role/aspect/importance/source/permission metadata uses the existing exact contract.
All media remains illustrative and preview-only in a random disposable schema.
The runtime is read-only and scoped to that schema. Ordinary production refuses FPO
and associated overlap, with the Home falling back to orientation. This fallback is
a publication safety boundary, not the proposed visual direction.

Essential illustration/report roles use authored visuals and readable text. Featured
evidence is wide 5:2; supporting evidence landscape 3:2; artifact metadata is portrait
4:5 inside a wider annotated composition. Context/contribution and rights remain
unresolved. These visual positions do not implement a production evidence collection.

accepted; future revisions still require outcome review. Automation
establishes functional/regression evidence, not aesthetic success.
