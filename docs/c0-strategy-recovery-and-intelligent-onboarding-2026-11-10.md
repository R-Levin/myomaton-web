## Business diagnosis and preliminary Presence Direction

A startup with a real offer may need an understandable first presence and a clear handoff for early inquiries. Its likely trigger is a launch or a growing referral stream without a credible destination. Its likely objection is paying for an ongoing relationship before knowing which work it will actually use. An established local service, professional or technical business may already have referrals and a website, yet still struggle to explain fit or keep the site accurate. Likely triggers include a changed offering, fragmented handoff or overdue replacement. Its likely objections concern interruption, loss of control and paying for a redesign that brings no continuing help. These triggers and objections are hypotheses. Seek a permission-safe example from each buying situation before sharpening acquisition copy. [Audience and scope](miopages-c0-product-model.md#initial-customer-hypothesis) · [October discovery](miopages-c0-business-brief.md#business-customer-and-geography)

**Positioning proposal.** Help an owner establish or improve how prospects find, understand and contact the business, then take responsibility for supported work that keeps that presence useful. Explain Standard Launch through business understanding, a bounded site, a visitor path and a measurement baseline where supported. Explain Ongoing through an observable cycle: notice a meaningful issue, prioritize it, confirm any new business fact, implement an approved change and assess the evidence available afterward. The customer owns business truth, authorization and consequential approvals. MioPages handles supported execution. Scope material supported migration or integration as Expanded only when warranted. Good fit depends on a clear offer or credible launch plan, owner participation and work within supported scope. A DIY builder, bespoke application, unsupported complex commerce or guaranteed lead or revenue result belongs outside the default promise. [Service deliverables and boundaries](miopages-c0-product-model.md#launch-and-ongoing-customer-deliverable-summary)

**Proposed page decisions.** Use Home to explain the value of a first launch or a handoff from an existing site, then show the same continuing service. Use Service to make responsibilities, exclusions and one ordinary Ongoing cycle tangible. Use Experience to separate verified historical operator experience from a clearly labeled current-workflow demonstration, adding outcome claims only when measured and attributable. Use Review to show what a prospect gets, how to qualify for a useful Review and which paths are available next. Keep the accepted C0 design as comparison evidence and do not carry old marketing or visual assets forward by default. [C0 site inventory and assessment](c0-adaptive-onboarding-walkthrough.md#the-existing-site-review)

## Generated-question storyboard

These are proposed outputs of an evidence-conditioned interviewer, with **simulated answers** to demonstrate what would change. They are neither owner answers nor outputs of the current app. The interviewer should surface an already supplied fact, ask about one consequential unknown and generate its next question from the latest answer. Ask for examples or records only when they can change the plan. Keep the rationale and evidence inspectable without making the customer manage a form.

### C0 chain 1 · What actually carries into Ongoing?

**Uses** the included-work response, the approved Launch/Ongoing scope and the startup correction. **Gap:** a supported example that makes continuing work tangible before any result can be claimed.

1. **Generated question:** “Suppose a startup’s first inquiries after Launch show that prospects misunderstand who the service is for. What would you normally do in Ongoing? What would you need from the owner before changing anything?”
2. **Simulation:** “I would compare the inquiries with the owner’s intended fit. I can propose clearer wording, but the owner has to confirm a new exclusion.”
3. **Generated follow-up from that answer:** “If inquiry notes aren’t available yet, what evidence is enough to propose a wording change, and which claims would you leave untouched until the owner confirms them?”
4. **Assessment change:** add a provisional first-cycle example that separates observation, proposal and authority. Hold lead-quality claims when evidence is thin. If the owner instead describes a supported routine correction requiring no new business fact, ask what identifies it as routine and who handles it. Then illustrate that smaller workflow.

### C0 chain 2 · When the Review is useful but its preview is weak

**Uses** the saved Review handoff and route intentions, the approved-undelivered earlier Review, and the owner direction to attempt an illustrative preview by default. **Gap:** approval and delivery fallback for distinct prospect paths.

1. **Generated question:** “If the written Review has two grounded priorities but the Home concept would misrepresent the business, what should the prospect receive, and which next step should still be available?”
2. **Simulation:** “The Operator should exclude the concept and approve the written Review. The prospect can request a conversation or choose to start.”
3. **Generated follow-up from that answer:** “When that prospect chooses Start, which facts already in the written Review should travel into fit and scope, and what remains necessary before anyone can confirm the commercial commitment?”
4. **Assessment change:** model an Operator-approved written Review without the excluded image, then continue with reused evidence and separate commercial approval. If the Operator instead holds delivery pending missing evidence, generate a question about what unlocks delivery and who follows up. Keep that Review unavailable until the requirement is met. Record any channel details only through the protected workflow.

### Fictional C2 chain · Emergency intent and routine estimates

**Uses** the benchmark's old-site observations of tree care and emergency calls, plus missing availability and no supplied Review evidence. **Gap:** truthful call coverage and a distinct non-urgent route.

1. **Generated question:** “Your old site mentions urgent tree work and routine estimates. In which areas and hours can someone actually answer an urgent call?”
2. **Simulation:** “We can answer weekday calls in part of the service area. At other times calls may reach voicemail.”
3. **Generated follow-up from that answer:** “What should the page tell a caller outside those hours or that area, and which route should someone use for a routine estimate?”
4. **Assessment change:** show a limited call path only after verifying the actual destinations, hours, coverage and approved fallback. Keep routine estimates separate. If the owner says emergency work has stopped, retire the claim and generate a follow-up about what old wording or links need investigation. There is no 24/7 claim and no invented Review. Resume the same displayed question. If a later correction changes applicability, retain the earlier exchange and explain what is now open. The Operator can suppress an irrelevant question or route an ambiguous claim for confirmation.
