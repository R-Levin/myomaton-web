# Full paid onboarding: customer business knowledge transfer contract

**Status: corrective product direction after C0 owner walkthrough, November 8–9, 2026.** This contract supersedes the fixed ten-step questionnaire interpretation in `commercial-v1-full-onboarding-questionnaire.md` and the `paid-onboarding/1` prototype's implied coverage. The current prototype remains a functioning **technical rehearsal of form/resume/submission mechanics**, but its content is **NOT an accepted full Commercial V1 onboarding interview**. It is not a sufficient build basis. These requirements are an owner direction, not implementation or permission to change production state.

## Objective: transfer real usable knowledge, not finish a short form

The post-commitment customer onboarding process should be like a well-designed **doctor's-office intake with a knowledgeable follow-up conversation**: prepared in advance; broad enough to discover the customer's situation; specific enough to capture the information needed; responsive to past answers; respectful of time; and comfortable for a customer who has extensive material to share. There is **no fixed maximum count of questions or universal ten-screen limit**. Ask more where business complexity or gaps warrant more, and fewer where trustworthy answers already exist. **Do not impose a completion quota or make customers fill irrelevant generic fields.** Important unanswered facts and potential opportunities must not be suppressed to meet an artificial interaction count.

The purpose is to acquire enough **accurate, structured, source-backed customer-specific knowledge** to assess their situation; understand their business, offerings, audience and customer decision journey; propose meaningful site/conversion/content direction; and produce a near-finished WordPress Managed Site under normal approvals. A mere business name, short offering paragraph, audience, region, and ten shallow prompts do not satisfy this purpose.

After onboarding, the customer should feel that MioPages has **thoroughly understood the business and its needs** and can take responsibility for doing the Web work. No ordinary customer should be asked to write a sitemap, choose Gutenberg blocks or manually prepare each page.

## Continuity, sequence and information reuse

- **One continuing intake, two optional entry paths:** The Free Review is **optional**, never a requirement for purchase. A Review-led prospect brings prior foundational questions/answers, research and delivered assessment into paid onboarding. A prospect who chooses **direct start** (or an assisted direct start) still needs the **foundational discovery interview** followed by the **deeper paid onboarding interview**, but they are two progressive depths of **one shared, resumable intake**, not separate or duplicative questionnaires. Screen/fit/scope and valid commercial authorization precede paid production discovery; there is **no mandatory free assessment or Free Review delivery** on direct start. Gather whatever essential foundation is not yet known, reuse existing-site research and every prior response, then ask only build-relevant deeper questions. A customer should never re-enter the same facts because they skipped the Free Review.
- **Two pre-onboarding evidence streams when a Free Review occurred:** carry forward both (1) the **existing-site audit / relevant public research**, including URLs, observed text, old offers, content gaps, form/contact routes and proposed keep/revise/retire/exclude dispositions, and (2) the **prospect's actual Free Review interview answers, clarifications, immutable submission, operator observations and delivered Review assessment**. Present a reconciled, source-labeled starting understanding rather than asking a second generic interview. Answers are attributed customer claims; website content remains observed/unverified; Review recommendations are interpretations, not owner-confirmed truth. Identify contradictions, staleness, omissions, and specifically deeper paid-onboarding gaps. The Free Review is not merely a few reusable form fields: its assessment and conversation are substantial reusable evidence. A direct-start customer may have no prior Review; handle that path without fabricating one. The initial Free Review stays appropriately short, distinct from deep paid onboarding.
- **Prepare before asking.** Systematically inventory earlier intake, prior discussions, the approved C0 three-pass discovery, customer-provided material and permissible public site/profiles. Source/date/confirmation/confidence must survive. Public research is *observation* and needs confirmation for factual truth, commercial claims, rights or ownership.
### Owner direction: lightweight market intelligence before questions

**November 10, 2026 — owner product direction; implementation not yet demonstrated.** MioPages serves businesses with something to sell to an identifiable market, including startups with no established site. Their industry/product/service category, customer problems, alternatives, expectations and competing Web presences provide useful **non-proprietary background context**. The AI should exploit the speed of a **small, proportionate public-research pass** to gain practical category literacy *before* demanding that the owner explain routine market facts. This generalizes the existing-site audit; it is not confined to nearby competitors or the customer's municipality.

For the category and intended market, examine a few appropriate organic search results and comparable businesses across relevant geographies, along with customer-oriented search intents and useful public sources where warranted: common offerings and boundaries, decision concerns, service/cost factors, trust signals, objections/complaints, and expected contact or conversion paths. For example, a tree-care operator's stated weekday urgent coverage should be considered against storm-damage queries, power-line safety and after-hours intent—**as hypotheses for owner clarification, never a factual claim about that operator or a safety promise**. Competitor prominence in search is one discovery signal, **not proof of business results, service quality, conversion effectiveness or what a customer is allowed to claim**. Do not copy competitor text, designs or original assets; category knowledge and independently synthesized patterns are not proprietary content. **Owner clarification, November 10: the research-derived basis and all downstream content direction must be a genuinely original amalgam of general category understanding, multiple independently observed patterns where useful, and this customer's confirmed knowledge—not a collage, close paraphrase, imitation of a distinctive competitor presentation, or transfer of third-party creative expression.** Generic facts, ordinary market conventions and common customer concerns can inform strategy; source references remain traceable for verification, but the output's wording, selection, structure, narrative and visual direction should be newly developed for this business. Do not reuse competitor testimonials, photos, graphics, brand assets or distinctive copy without appropriate rights. Flag obvious near-matches for revision before approval; do not burden initial exploration with legalistic rituals, and do not claim that AI generation alone guarantees non-infringement. Distinguish organic results from advertisements where observable, and acknowledge search ranking variation.

Turn the short research result into an explainable comparative understanding: **what the owner has said; what existing evidence shows; what customers appear to seek; what other providers commonly present; where there is a meaningful discrepancy or opportunity; and what is still unverified**. Select and freshly formulate the next question by its likely **consequence for a real Web Presence decision** (Offering/scope, positioning, trust/proof, primary and secondary visitor actions, search/content structure, qualification, operating promises or readiness), not just by an empty questionnaire field. Ask only where the answer has value, then reconsider priorities after it arrives. This is an **intelligence/quality requirement**, not approval for unlimited crawling, ungrounded generalizations, third-party content reuse, indiscriminate collection, or extra operator bureaucracy. Right-size source breadth, latency and expense to likely value; expose provenance and uncertainty. The exact research/query strategy and question-ranking rubric still need design and an owner-visible C0/C2/startup comparison before implementation acceptance.

- **What we understand / what we need:** Start the paid interview by presenting consolidated existing knowledge and proposed understanding for targeted confirmation or correction. Show known information without redundant “What do you offer?”/“Who are your customers?” prompts. For previously confirmed items, ask for *materially deeper detail* only where needed to produce content or understand conversion.
- **No fake original questions.** Some October 5–6 C0 prompts and earlier synthesis artifacts survive; a complete literal transcript is unverified. The October 14 Free Review is separate. Evidence provenance remains truthful; fresh corrections are new records.
- **Generated follow-ups, not only preselected fields.** Use the known business facts, site/public-presence evidence, prior Free Review, actual owner replies and structured coverage gaps to **formulate new, business-specific questions** when they would materially improve understanding or build readiness. Prewritten questions, templates and rules can be starting points and constraints, but **the interview must not be limited to selecting a question from a finite preset bank**. Tailor questions and their conditional follow-ups to the customer's actual offerings, scope, operational realities, visitor decisions and answers; ask more or fewer as warranted. Record the reason/gap, source context, exact question/version, answer, provenance, and subsequent applicability in the authorized resumable session. Don't repeat known facts, fabricate service details, ask speculative questions as though assumptions are confirmed, or emit unrelated industry's choices (e.g. emergency tree-service options in C0 MioPages). On contradictory or regulated/high-consequence information, route to an Operator/customer confirmation rather than treating generated interpretation as business truth. Operator can intervene, revise or suppress inappropriate prompts. Demonstrate true new-question generation (not just fixed conditional display), durable resume, normalization and at least one second-order follow-up in a bounded test before accepting full adaptive interviewing.
- **Comprehensive capture, progressive presentation.** Use a readable topical outline, expandable sections, optional rich elaboration, short initial prompts that branch to meaningful follow-ups, auto-save/save-and-return, visible missing-information summary, and help. Customer-provided documents, media, existing-site links and free-text explanation should be supported as evidence channels when actual safe ingestion/upload is implemented. Never claim an unimplemented upload or research feature works.
- **Material unknowns get resolved.** The system may infer directions for review, but must not silently invent pricing, qualifications, claims, commitments, reviews, testimonials, rights, products or promises. The customer can say “not sure,” with appropriate follow-up/support and launch-readiness classification.
- **Complete enough for a site vs complete every optional answer:** The full interview remains open to richer input even after minimum site facts are known. A knowledge-readiness assessment, not a fixed number of answered questions, determines when the operator can recommend a plan. Optional detail can continue without blocking progress; key missing facts are explicit exceptions.

## Required business-knowledge coverage (adapted per business)

For **every applicable topic** below, support as much specificity as the customer can reasonably provide. These are **coverage domains**, not an instruction to ask every business every question or to overwhelm them with one page of fields. Use structured repeatable objects (e.g., multiple products/services, locations, teams, proof examples) with sensible free-text and file/reference support.

| Area | Information worth capturing | Consequence for assessment or site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Business and market** | Identity and actual trading name; history and experience; purpose; current services, customers and operational scope; what is changing; goals, priorities, constraints, near-term plans | Accurate positioning, essential About/Company information, fit and initial plan |
| **Offerings in depth** | Each priority product/service/program; what it is and isn't; customers served; problems solved; practical outcomes and use cases; distinct variants, eligibility, process/delivery, availability, price/range/quote policy when publishable, policies/limits and priority | Genuine service/product overview and, where warranted, individual detail Pages—not generic “we offer X” paragraphs |
| **Audience and decision making** | Key segments; whom not to target; problems/triggers; what customers ask before engaging; objections, alternatives and reasons they choose this provider; terminology/customary industry vocabulary; common misunderstandings | Relevant answers, trust, SEO/discovery ideas, CTA progression and meaningful page architecture |
| **Differentiation and evidence** | Actual competitive advantages and how substantiated; certifications, memberships, process, staff experience, project examples, testimonials, reviews, photographs, outcomes and evidence ownership/permissions | Credible claims and supporting content rather than invented slogans, superlatives or results |
| **Conversion and contact experience** | Desired visitor outcomes; what makes a qualified lead; calls, email, forms, estimates, appointments, bookings, buying elsewhere; contact recipients/routing, response expectations, business hours, exclusions and constraints | Working contact/conversion paths and measurement, not decorative CTAs |
| **Geography, locations and operations** | Service territory, individual offices/locations, appointment rules, hours, remote vs on-site work, region-specific offerings, seasonal limits and regulatory/eligibility constraints | Location presentation, appropriate routing, geography/SEO and disclaimers |
| **Business personality and presentation** | Voice, tone, language, important claims/phrases and terminology, brand history, current assets, visual examples liked/disliked, accessibility/language needs and meaningful constraints | A reviewable Visual Direction proposal, not customer-selected CSS/layout mechanics |
| **Current website / content / history** | Existing site(s), assets, pages, posts, downloads, URLs, media, lead systems, analytics, what actually works/doesn't, outstanding problems and customer preferences | Audited content/migration decisions, continuity, redirects, proof and fresh-build priorities |
| **Existing web ecosystem** | Domains/DNS/email ownership and continuity, profiles/listings, external sites, booking/CRM/ecommerce, forms, social channels, SEO/Search Console/analytics access status and relevant integrations | Supported launch architecture, risk/ownership/consent inventory; specialized setup checked later |
| **Content resources and future opportunities** | Topics customers need explained; existing articles/FAQ/material; product sheets, presentations, brochures, policies, photos, videos; planned additions/events/campaigns, common customer questions | Initial launch content and realistic next opportunities, avoiding filler or arbitrary quotas |
| **Regulated or consequential information** | Privacy, industry claims, licenses, safety/financial/health claims, mandatory legal text, special approval process, proof and permitted publishing boundaries where applicable | Accurate claims, review assignment, exclusions and launch gates |
| **People, workflows, approval and timing** | Who can supply/approve facts; who receives leads; necessary approvals/permissions, ownership of assets/systems, desired launch constraints; contact route for ongoing changes | Permissions/realistic scope and delivery plan, without selling dedicated account management |

### Existing-site inventory: explicitly assess retention **and rejection**

Do **not** ask only “What should we keep?” The system must help the customer evaluate what should be:
1. **Keep as is** (after ownership, accuracy and link verification);
2. **Keep but improve/rewrite** (content, structure, presentation or conversions);
3. **Replace entirely** (new message, asset or page);
4. **Remove or retire** (outdated, ineffective, duplicate, incorrect or unwanted material);
5. **Explicitly NOT carry forward** (customer rejects a feature, wording, branding, photograph, claim, service, offer, site section or inherited practice);
6. **Investigate/undecided** (important value/authority unresolved).

A suggested inventory from the existing site (pages, menus, posts, PDFs, links, images, offers, CTAs, SEO destinations and key URLs) should **prepopulate** these decisions after permitted inspection. Present the customer a brief, scannable review rather than making them enumerate every URL manually. Follow up: “What frustrates you about the current site?”, “What should never appear on the new site?”, “Are there old services, claims or images we should retire?”, “Which links or ranking pages must continue working?” Capture specific and general negative preferences, reasons and exceptions. Customer feedback on what must **not** be retained has first-class standing in the proposed design/content and must not be silently overwritten by import, AI copy or old C0 accepted previews. Record redirect decisions/technical verification separately.

For brand references and competitor examples also capture **both liked and disliked traits**, with reasons; a URL is not permission to clone a design or copy third-party content.

### Guided depth: examples (not an exhaustive mandatory script)

If the customer says “We install commercial AV systems,” follow up on installation types, target customers, planning vs installation vs managed support, geographic coverage, vendor relationships, typical client challenges, when a lead is qualified, proof/case studies, and any unsupported claims to avoid—drawing first from their existing site or documents rather than asking the whole set blindly.

If the customer has three major services, the system should be able to explore **each** sufficiently to generate meaningful Pages. If a 25-person company has dozens of offerings or locations, show structured grouping and bulk material/import options instead of forcing a single text field. For a simpler business, do not demand enterprise complexity.

Where answers are vague (e.g. “We offer quality service” or “Everyone is our customer”), follow up constructively with examples, customer stories and narrower questions; do not treat a generic answer as confirmed enough for a publishable promise. When the user offers rich detail voluntarily, capture and structure it rather than truncating it to match predefined fields.

## Customer experience and readiness

The interface should feel like a prepared, attentive interviewer. Encourage detail without requiring essays: short prompts, contextual multiple choice when helpful, optional long-form text, attach/reference supporting documents and clear “tell us more” opportunities. Avoid artificial ten-step pacing. Show sections completed, what remains materially unknown and the **resulting understanding**, not just “9 of 10.” Pausing and resuming must preserve exact answers and source evidence.

A responsive interview needs an actual **coverage/knowledge-gap engine** (rules and later optional AI-assisted interpretation) tied to the **intended site**, not a flat list of 10 generic fields. It must distinguish:
- facts known and confirmed;
- facts present but needing correction/verification or greater detail;
- missing material information;
- optional enrichment;
- questions rendered irrelevant by previous answers;
- customer-declared exclusions and rejected old material.

Don't declare “complete” solely because a fixed sequence has been traversed. At the end, present:
1. **Initial assessment of the business and current Web presence**: what the system knows, observed strengths/weaknesses, intended audience/conversion, main risks/opportunities, with uncertainty and source clarity; actual website audit findings only if a real inspection occurred.
2. **Business knowledge organized for site generation**, including substantive offering detail, audience and proof information; a no-go list of prohibited/retired material; gaps needing clarification.
3. **A concise, focused set of consequential follow-up questions** where necessary, now that comprehensive material has been ingested.
4. A recommendation for **Presence Direction** and Page/content/conversion architecture only when evidence is sufficient, reviewed through the existing business-understanding checkpoint before construction.

In Commercial V1, a knowledgeable Operator may assist at any point or help interpret material, but regular collection must be designed to minimize Operator time and customer effort. Avoid a fire hose while **not artificially limiting knowledge intake**.

## C0 implementation correction and acceptance gates

The **November 8 `paid-onboarding/1` ten-step UI (commit `4672bfa`) is a developer-functional smoke proof, not full onboarding product acceptance.** Its five approved-summary prefills and testing of scoped drafts/resume/immutable submissions are useful. Its one-field-per-area approach, untested depth, forced “routine updates preference” and sequence-complete submission are **rejected as full onboarding design** following the owner's actual walkthrough. The owner specifically observed it **felt like the initial interview** and failed to ask the types of questions needed for an initial assessment.

**Next work before more code**: compare the current implementation against these coverage domains and the pre-sale Free Review fields. Produce an explicit missing-information matrix for MioPages C0 and one **reviewable substantive interview script/experience design** illustrating how known C0 answers are skipped, deep offering/audience/current-site information is elicited, positive **and negative** keep/remove decisions captured, and a meaningful assessment follows. Then implement/test the revised adaptive experience in bounded slices, with at least one real follow-up branch and evidence-bound upload/reference handling where implemented. Keep the preserved owner-run submission intact; do not automatically erase or overwrite the C0 rehearsal or committed data.

**Acceptance is knowledge-based:**
- Starting from the genuine C0 approved discovery, the system **does not ask again for baseline business name/offer/audience/region** unless requesting specifically deeper or corrected information;
- asks useful **new, detailed questions** sufficient to turn C0's offerings, audience questions and actual scope into reasonable proposed site copy and structure;
- discovers **what should *not* be kept** from the legacy MioPages website in addition to what matters;
- allows comprehensive, context-dependent answers and new offerings/proof/material without a fixed question-count cap;
- identifies unresolved truth/claims and shows an initial source-grounded assessment and actionable site/build knowledge, not merely an answer dump;
- supports pause/resume, provenance, change/approval safeguards and privacy within existing trusted architecture;
- demonstrates the **customer's confidence in the site's fidelity to their business** before approving a site plan.

Do not move to Core-to-WordPress generation on the strength of the ten-step prototype. There is no new production publication, account activation, billing, customer-state migration or credential authorization in this correction.
