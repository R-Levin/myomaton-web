## Business, customer and geography

MioPages is the customer-facing commercial brand for one managed Web Presence
service. Initial customers are established, expertise-led small service or technical
businesses with real customers and expertise, but a weak, outdated, fragmented or
neglected presence. Approximately 3–25 people is a planning profile, not an
eligibility limit. They have little appetite for DIY web management or a costly,
bloated or opaque traditional agency engagement.

Initial geography is remote/national United States. International work requires
deliberate later evaluation. The customer problem extends beyond a new website:
clearer business/service explanation, credibility and trust, discoverability,
better lead/conversion paths, continuing attention/improvement and less technical
or administrative burden.

MioPages aims to be more capable/accountable than DIY and more streamlined/measurable
than an unsuitable traditional agency model. This is not a claim that all agencies
are bad. Design should be professional, current, purposeful, conversion-aware and
visually strong without pixel-perfect obsession; design serves business outcomes.
**Complexity may scale; customization does not.**

Customers supply business truth, goals, preferences, Assets, necessary account
authority and approval. They do not direct arbitrary implementation mechanics.
The outcome is that the right prospects can find the business, understand its
offer, see its relevance, trust it and take the next useful step.

## Web Presence Review

The Review is free initially and useful independently of a sale. The provisional
target is approximately **two business days after minimum information is available**.
It returns what MioPages understands, apparent obstacles/problems, roughly three
useful priorities and a recommended next path. It may conclude that MioPages is
not a fit. It is not a free full-strategy engagement or custom consulting project.

Continuation can be a short conversation, continued self-service onboarding, or
Standard/Expanded Launch confirmation. These are service paths, not three unrelated
products or a commitment that their UI/workflows already exist.

## Self-service and smallest useful intake

Self-service means the customer supplies information/preferences without needing
an intake person; the system synthesizes and proposes a bounded solution. It does
not mean DIY page building, arbitrary design control or customer-directed layouts.

```text
compact intake → AI synthesis → customer/operator correction
→ progressive enrichment → approved business brief
```

AI synthesis remains a proposal until confirmed. The smallest useful first pass is:

- Identity/contact, what the business offers and who it serves.
- Service area and desired visitor/business Action.
- Most important current-presence problem.
- Existing website/public profiles.
- Consequential external-system or operational constraints.

Discover where possible, infer then confirm where appropriate, and ask directly
when the customer/operator is authoritative. Inferred facts cannot silently become
approved business truth. The [onboarding contract](onboarding-optimization.md)
owns trusted confirmation and progressive knowledge updates.

Current serious direction is clear public pricing, a fixed Standard Launch, fixed
bounded complexity increments and one primary monthly Ongoing service. Avoid normal
hourly/custom quoting and deceptive “starting at” pricing. Expanded Launch receives
a fixed confirmed price after Review; third-party costs should be transparent.

| Provisional list amount | Current hypothesis |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Launch | Approximately **$1,095**. |
| Ongoing | Approximately **$249/month**. |

Amounts are hypotheses until actual delivery validates them, not final commercial
terms or platform invariants. Founding-customer discounts/credits may be explicit
adjustments without redefining the provisional list pricing.

Launch is separate from Ongoing; Standard Launch is likely paid upfront. Ongoing
begins at go-live, is month-to-month, and currently anticipates approximately
30 days' cancellation notice. Exact payment/cancellation wording remains unresolved.
Preserve clean customer ownership/portability and retention through value, not lock-in.

An initial outside cohort of approximately **5–10 customers** should prove onboarding
effort, repeatable generalization, delivery timing/quality, customer experience,
report usefulness, operator workload, pricing sustainability, retention, measurable
outcomes and service economics. Some operator assistance is expected during proving.
Automate where proven; use human judgment where uncertainty still carries risk.
The cohort size is a planning hypothesis, not an eligibility or platform limit.

## Real service identity and infrastructure

MioPages is the brand/service, not a personality-led solo consultancy. Do not
fabricate a larger organization. Expose real functions: **Client Management**,
**Content Review**, **Technical Operations**, **Measurement & Optimization**, and
**Service Administration**. One real person may perform several functions initially;
these labels are not claims of separate employees or implemented permission roles.
No artifice or false organizational structure; assign real people as needed.

Known MioPages assets from confirmed discovery are its domain, 20i reseller hosting,
email, X and Facebook. X/Facebook are mostly stub presence assets. GA4 and Search
Console account/property status is pending verification. No Google Business Profile
is currently confirmed. Exact public identity/contact values still need verification.

20i provides infrastructure leverage, not product identity. Hosting, domains, DNS
and email can be coordinated where required without becoming the primary marketed
products. A few legacy hosting customers are separate from the new offer and should
retain practical access to existing services where possible. Portal/account details
require later evaluation, not an invented integration or automatic migration.

## Integrations and measurement boundaries

No bespoke customer-only integration by default. Specialized services can use
reusable bounded supported patterns. Classify new requests as core capability,
supported optional integration, future roadmap, partner/referral or outside scope.
A customer request informs product development but does not automatically purchase
custom development. The [operations model](service-operations-model.md#supported-external-integrations)
owns the decision framework.

First-party measurement is the intended independent operational core. GA4 and
Search Console are preferred external intelligence where available/proven; their
service-account/property-grant model remains an unproven near-term spike. GBP is
future-reference only. Provider failure must not disable independent core operation.
The [growth loop](content-growth-loop.md) owns ongoing knowledge/content work, and
the measurement architecture owns source availability and attribution limitations.
