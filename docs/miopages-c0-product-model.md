# MioPages Customer 0 product model

## Purpose and status

MioPages is both Customer #0, a deeper internal proving ground for the Web Presence
platform, and a realistic candidate for the commercial home from which the service
will eventually be offered. For development/generalization, it can also stand in
for Customer #2: the first deliberate non-robotics case after Myomaton Customer #1.
These are proving roles, not separate architectures or assertions that corresponding
customer records have been created.

MioPages already exists as a real but largely moribund web-presence business/site.
Its existing content is historical input, not canonical product direction. Its
positioning, offer, content and design are open to reconsideration through trusted
operator decisions. This document defines a product hypothesis and proving purpose;
it does not launch the service, migrate the existing site or write customer state.

The [Web Presence service model](web-presence-service-model.md) owns the reusable
service independent of this business. The [capability catalog](platform-capabilities.md)
and [implementation register](deferred-architecture.md) own implementation status
and gates. MioPages requirements should expose reusable needs rather than create
another manually crafted one-off website.

## Historical and operational context

The following is operator-supplied context, not a platform invariant:

- MioPages is tied to an existing 20i reseller account; current content reflects
  that hosting/reseller relationship.
- Initial production deployment may likely use 20i infrastructure. Provider
  suitability and the integration/portal boundary still need review.
- Social accounts and other external presence assets may already exist. Google
  Business Profile or a similar presence may also exist; inventory and ownership
  confirmation are needed before treating them as known active channels.
- MioPages historically served primarily as a marketing site. Reseller/customer
  infrastructure was separate from that public presentation.
- The Mio / Myo naming lineage provides some incidental shared historical DNA;
  it does not define customer messaging or product architecture.

20i is provisional infrastructure/provisioning leverage, not the product definition.
The service should own business knowledge, onboarding, approval and optimization
regardless of hosting provider. Existing reseller content or account structures
must not become assumptions every future customer inherits. See the
[service provider boundary](web-presence-service-model.md#20i-and-infrastructure-boundary).

## Initial customer hypothesis

The initial Ideal Customer Profile is an established small service business,
roughly 3–25 people, with real expertise, existing customers and a legitimate
offering. Its web presence is outdated, weak, fragmented or neglected. It has
little desire to become a webmaster and limited internal capacity for sustained
web/content optimization. Traditional agency cost or complexity may feel
uncomfortable or disproportionate.

Candidate categories include trades, professional services, niche B2B, local
service businesses, small agencies and small nonprofits/organizations. These are
discovery targets, not industry restrictions. The approximate organization size
is an acquisition hypothesis, not a platform limit or eligibility rule.

### Customer pains to test

- An outdated or embarrassing website with no clear internal owner.
- Slow/difficult updates and stale service or business information.
- An expensive, opaque or unresponsive agency relationship.
- Little measurable business value from the site.
- Search, content and social work that is always postponed.
- A fragmented presence across websites, accounts and tools.
- Concern that a redesign means another expensive project.
- Too much time on visual minutiae and too little on business outcomes.

Use these hypotheses to shape MioPages messaging and conditional
[onboarding](onboarding-optimization.md), then test them against actual customer
needs. Do not assume every customer experiences every pain or that analytics alone
explains the business problem.

## Positioning and intended outcome

The intended middle ground is more capable and accountable than DIY, and more
streamlined and measurable than a traditional agency engagement. Customers should
not need to become webmasters or buy an overbuilt process with high cost,
unnecessary complexity, lengthy subjective revisions and unclear business return.
This contrasts with a model unsuited to these customers, not with all agencies;
specialist agencies can be the appropriate option for other requirements.

The presence should help the right prospects find the business, understand its
offer, see its relevance/difference, trust it and take the next useful step toward
becoming a lead/customer/client where appropriate. A launched site is a service
deliverable. Business understanding and useful conversion are the intended outcome,
not guaranteed sales or a claimed conversion-rate improvement before measurement.

## Design serves the business

Design matters when it serves the business. Seek purposeful, professional, current
design that improves clarity, differentiation, trust, discoverability and conversion.
Spend effort where it produces value. Strong visual quality remains essential;
bounded does not mean visually conservative.

Reject pixel-perfect obsession for its own sake, endless subjective revision,
novelty without purpose and design theater that consumes budget without improving
outcomes. Customers approve representative outcomes rather than direct every
spacing value, shadow, layout and font size. Exhaustive pixel-level control is
outside the intended fit, not an unpromised service delivered through custom CSS.

MioPages should use the [Design System / Visual Direction](visual-direction.md)
with an appropriate direction distinct from Myomaton. No profile, palette or Page
composition is selected by this document. Limited imagery should still yield a
professional result through supported composition and typography, not copied
reference layouts, arbitrary controls or customer-specific rendering hacks.

## Continuing value after launch

Launch starts the relationship. Retention should come from useful ongoing work:
content recommendations, grounded drafts and human-approved publication,
measurement, optimization, business-knowledge updates, better service/product
representation and clearer conversion paths. Later channel/distribution extensions
can add value without being promised as launch capabilities.

The [content-growth loop](content-growth-loop.md) owns the detailed opportunity,
knowledge-gathering, approval, distribution and measurement contracts. MioPages
should prove that loop with commercial topics and lead-generation intent. Continued
service must earn its place through understandable work and measurable learning or
improvement; technical lock-in is not a retention strategy.

## What Customer 0 must prove

| Proving area | Reusable question |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial positioning | Can the platform explain a service business and its value without robotics-specific assumptions? |
| Service/product explanation | Can trusted business information produce appropriate Pages, Navigation and internal progression? |
| Contact and conversion | Can valid Actions and an appropriate Contact path support useful next steps within production gates? |
| Trust/proof | Can supported content explain credibility without invented claims or pretending the planned Testimonial domain exists? |
| Limited imagery and design | Can a different bounded Visual Direction produce professional, intentional results with sparse Assets? |
| Intake | Can existing business information be reviewed, corrected and turned into trusted canonical knowledge rather than blindly copied? |
| External presence | Can accounts/profiles be inventoried with confirmed versus unknown ownership/status and realistic management scope? |
| Content growth | Can a small set of commercial content opportunities lead to targeted questions and useful approved drafts? |
| Infrastructure | Can a likely 20i deployment use repeatable operations without coupling the domain model to a reseller panel? |
| Measurement and optimization | Can observations become comprehensible recommendations, with uncertainty and effort visible? |

Use existing capabilities first. A genuine recurring gap should enter the
[capability/gate review](deferred-architecture.md), not be hidden in MioPages-only
code. Existing content can inform intake, but the platform is not committed to
ingesting, cloning or reproducing the old site. No automated onboarding, analytics,
publishing or external integration is claimed by defining these proving tests.

## Shared human and AI operating philosophy

MioPages/Web Presence shares operating DNA with Myomaton / Open Practical Robotics,
not robotics requirements. Humans contribute purpose, business/domain knowledge,
judgment, values, priority, continuity, approval and responsibility. AI/system
assistance contributes synthesis, structure, pattern identification, exploration,
drafting, implementation leverage, measurement interpretation and coordination.

Important knowledge and decisions remain externalized and reviewable. AI has no
independent business authority or responsibility; conversational memory is not the
canonical repository. The [content-growth philosophy](content-growth-loop.md#human-and-system-contributions)
and service responsibility split govern practical application.

## Implementation boundaries

- **Implemented now:** existing Managed Site and structured rendering foundations,
  as cataloged in [platform capabilities](platform-capabilities.md). Myomaton's
  reference-v2 application is a proving result, not a prebuilt MioPages customer.
- **Planned core / pre-launch:** MioPages-specific reviewed intake/positioning and
  an appropriate presence; repeatable onboarding, production operation, measurement
  and content-growth workflows subject to their existing gates.
- **Future extensions:** automated distribution and richer external-profile/channel
  integrations. Existing accounts and the reseller relationship do not establish
  implemented connectors or authorize automated account operations.

Offer details, packaging, pricing, portal integration and deployment selection
remain to be validated. This model sets direction without prescribing a schema,
fixed commercial terms or a delivery commitment.
