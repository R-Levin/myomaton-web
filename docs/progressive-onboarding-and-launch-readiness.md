# Progressive onboarding and Launch Readiness

## Authority, continuity and status

**The Free Web Presence Review is the first stage of progressive onboarding, not
a separate intake system.** Review, direct start and assisted start share the same
foundational information and deepen it only as needed. The system does the homework
first; the customer supplies proprietary knowledge, corrections, priorities,
judgment and facts the system cannot safely know or infer.

Never ask again for information already held unless it needs confirmation because
it may have changed, materially greater detail is needed, or earlier information
is ambiguous/incomplete. Reuse answers with source, freshness and confirmation
status. Permitted public research provides evidence, not automatically trusted
business truth, asset rights, account ownership or authorization.

This document owns the complete progressive Review-to-launch workflow and readiness
model. The [customer journey](customer-journey-and-change-management.md) retains
approval/commercial handoff and business-change authority; [Conversion Architecture](conversion-architecture.md)
owns intent, Page roles and conversion paths; [onboarding/optimization](onboarding-optimization.md)
owns trusted intake/intelligence policy. The [service operating model](service-operations-model.md)
owns reusable scope, Ongoing effort and cadence; the [service proposition](web-presence-service-model.md)
owns outcomes/ownership. [Measurement](measurement-and-analytics.md),
[security/operations](security-operations.md), the [capability catalog](platform-capabilities.md)
and [gate register](deferred-architecture.md) retain their specialist contracts.
[MioPages C0](miopages-c0-product-model.md) proves the process without making its
prices, Pages or business-specific answers platform invariants.

| Status | Actual boundary |

## Provisional Free Review intake

The current standard intake is an implementation-ready baseline, not an immutable
questionnaire. Keep answers short, reuse/prefill supported known information for
confirmation and defer specialist questions. The goal-choice limit below organizes
priorities; it is not a platform-wide schema limit. Phone is not required.

| # | Question | Provisional answer structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What are you looking to do? | Improve/replace an existing presence; start from scratch; focused Campaign/addition alongside an existing presence. |
| 2 | What business or organization should we review? | Name and website/public presence where applicable. No existing site is a valid starting point. |
| 3 | What do you most want your Web presence to accomplish? | Choose up to three: qualified leads; clearer offer; better visibility; promote an offering/location/Campaign; replace ineffective/outdated presence; establish a professional presence; something else. |
| 4 | Who are you most trying to reach? | Short free text. |
| 5 | What do you offer? | Main products/services/programs. |
| 6 | Why do customers choose you? | Short free text; unknown or unproven differentiators remain unresolved. |
| 7 | What is the biggest problem with your current Web presence? | Use a starting-from-scratch equivalent when there is no current presence. |
| 8 | What would make this Review most useful to you? | Focus/priority in the prospect's words. |
| 9 | How would you ideally like prospects to respond? | Multiple selections: call; message; quote/estimate; consultation/appointment; schedule service; visit; buy/order elsewhere; register/sign up; other. Preferences do not establish operational availability. |
| 10 | Where do you serve customers? | Local; several cities/regions; statewide; nationwide; primarily online/location not material; other. Ask specific areas only when relevant. |
| 11 | Where should we deliver the Review? | Name and email; role/title optional. Phone not required. Delivery identity does not prove business/account ownership. |
| 12 | Anything else you would like us to know? | Optional. |

### Conditional questions and editorial language

Ask follow-ups only when business/Offering/intent evidence makes them material:
urgency/response times, multiple locations, ecommerce, CRM, booking, specialized
integrations, regulation, unusual qualification, large catalogs or seasonality.
Emergency plumbing may need realistic response-time and call/immediate-callback
questions; a consultant/training firm without that intent normally would not.
Default intake stays short; specialized questions need a reason, not a universal
sales questionnaire.

For future customer-facing copy, avoid “inquiry/inquiries” unless natural in context.
Prefer contact, message, request, lead, prospect or question according to meaning.
This is an editorial rule, not a blind replacement of technical terms, schemas or
existing content, and these terms are not interchangeable measurement events.

## Concise Review, approval and delivery

The Review demonstrates understanding and useful direction without performing paid
Launch work. Usually aim for one strong screen or roughly 1–2 pages, not a giant audit.

Recommended output: what we understand; what appears to be working (or an appropriate
starting-point framing for a new business); approximately **three primary opportunities**;
recommended first direction; a conversion observation; MioPages fit; optional Home
concept teaser; one forward Continue/Start with MioPages action; and subordinate
question/help access.

For each primary opportunity explain what matters, why and the recommended direction.
Do not supply finished Page copy, final sitemap, keyword spreadsheets, a complete
content calendar, final conversion specification, wireframes or implementation
instructions. Free value is diagnosis/direction; paid value executes it.

Initially every Review follows **system draft → operator review → operator approval
→ delivery**. Check business understanding, supporting evidence, opportunity priority,
usefulness, free/paid boundary, fit, tone and clarity. Operator approval authorizes
that Review's delivery, not canonical promotion of unconfirmed facts, asset rights
or customer publication. Existing internal operator-minute hypotheses are not SLAs.

The initial intended delivery is an email notification linking to a clean **private
Web Review Page**. After reading, offer one forward Continue/Start action with
secondary help, not a choice among competing purchase funnels. PDF/download can
come later if useful. Private access needs an explicit scoped, revocable access and
retention design under P7/P14; a hidden URL is not sufficient authorization. This
document chooses no authentication/link-token mechanism and implements no email.

### Optional experimental Home teaser

When enough evidence exists, an optional system-generated/operator-reviewed concept
may make capability tangible: Hero plus perhaps 1–2 representative Sections using
normal bounded Design System/presentation capabilities. Use business-appropriate
direction, not mandatory MioPages branding for every prospect. Label it illustrative,
not final copy/design, and apply existing preview/media eligibility rules.

It is not guaranteed or a free design engagement. Omit it if operator effort becomes
excessive. Experimentally record whether generated, operator minutes, prospect
engagement and whether conversion appears to improve, preserving uncertainty and
privacy. Do not claim causation from a few prospects or require this experiment
for every Review.

## Review and direct start converge

Review path: foundational information → Free Review → prospect elects to continue
→ reuse known information → fit/scope confirmation and commercial gate → deeper
paid onboarding.

Direct start: **same foundational questions/information** → skip Review delivery
→ fit/scope determination → same commercial gate → deeper paid onboarding.

Reuse known answers on either path; direct start does not force redundant re-entry
or buy an unseen scope. Assisted start guides this same process. Neither path is a
page builder or a second intake system, and a clicked link does not prove that a
Review, payment or prior information capture occurred.

## MioPages working commercial model

These are current MioPages working decisions/assumptions pending final commercial,
legal and accounting review, **not final terms, canonical pricing or public-price
eligibility**. They refine earlier non-numeric payment hypotheses for this business;
they do not change existing Offering/Pricing records or authorize public copy updates.

| Component | Current working model |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Launch | **$1,250 upfront**. Standard is the normal offer, not a teaser. |
| Launch installment option | **6 monthly payments of $225; $1,350 total**. Keep the Launch obligation distinct from Ongoing. |
| Ongoing | **$249/month, beginning at go-live**. Month-to-month working model with an approximately 30-day cancellation concept, subject to final terms. |
| Expanded Launch | Rare, materially unusual supported complexity; operator confirms scope and customer/operator agree price before commitment. |
| Campaign / major Revamp | Separately scoped when justified; not an automatic surcharge or permanent high-growth tier. |

Most businesses/organizations should fit Standard. Absorb normal effort variation
and minor complexity. Only materially unusual economics/operating burden, such as
a very large catalog, many locations or substantial unusual supported setup/integration,
justifies Expanded. No plan-tier creep, arbitrary customization or unsupported
integration is authorized. Earlier twelve-month payment speculation is not a
current offered option.

After the prospect elects to continue, the intended Standard commercial gate shows
recommended Standard scope, $1,250 upfront or 6 × $225, $249/month Ongoing from
go-live, the month-to-month working model and clear exclusions without surprise
extra work. **Accept/confirmed commercial commitment → deeper onboarding**, using
trusted confirmation rather than a browser assertion. Before activating this gate,
P26 must resolve agreement/payment mechanics; acceptance is not a claim that billing
exists or an instruction to collect payment now. Rare Expanded cases explain why
and confirm scope/price before commitment.

### Inclusive Ongoing and understandable exit

One broadly inclusive Ongoing service should convey “we've got you covered” without
promising unlimited labor. Absorb normal variation: routine business facts and
ordinary additions/removals/changes, content recommendations/preparation, core-site
and conversion improvements, reporting, optimization, operation/maintenance and
customer priorities within the service envelope.

If asked for more posts, assess usefulness and do more when justified within normal
Ongoing; recommend a different action if better. A genuinely accelerated initiative
materially exceeding the envelope may justify a bounded Campaign. Do not sell
post/Page quotas or an “8 posts/month” plan ladder. The
[opportunity/effort model](opportunity-and-optimization-model.md#effort-budgeting-and-campaign-escalation)
owns prioritization; routine included evolution must not quietly become paid expansion.

Working nonpayment/exit direction: unpaid work may pause; an unpaid Launch balance
remains due according to the eventual agreement. Neither is a finalized collection
or cancellation term. Do not weaponize customer data as leverage: customer-owned
information/accounts/assets remain theirs. Normal exit should provide a practical
export including MioPages-created content and useful reporting/history according
to final ownership/license/retention terms, without promising every historical copy.
Managed MioPages backend/platform access or IP does not transfer. Export remains
gated by P5/P6, not implemented here. Rare bad debt may be preferable to collections
drama; legal/accounting review must resolve actual treatment before use.

## Paid understanding, gap analysis and Presence Direction

After commitment, prepare **Here is what we understand about your business** from
intake, permitted public sites/profiles, Review analysis, known offers/audiences/goals,
conversion preferences, geography and supplied assets/material. Distinguish confirmed
facts from interpretations and open questions. The customer confirms, corrects,
adds missing knowledge and updates priorities; do not present a giant second form.
Confirmed corrections follow existing canonical-write/approval safeguards.

Explain that a few focused questions may follow review of those corrections. Ask
only when a missing answer materially affects accuracy, Page/content decisions,
Offering representation, conversion, trust/proof, capacity, technical operation or
Launch readiness. Possible gaps include proprietary facts, offer nuances, eligibility/
limitations, pricing/timing, objections, credentials, capacity/priorities, brand
terminology, integrations and assets. **If an answer would not materially change
what MioPages builds or says, do not ask.** Group related gaps into a few understandable
questions; unknowns are not permission to invent truth.

### One concise Presence Direction

With sufficient confirmed knowledge, prepare a decision document containing business
understanding, priority audiences/Offerings, goals, recommended Pages and purposes,
Conversion Architecture/intents/paths, content direction, proof/trust needs, Visual
Direction, technical/operational requirements and Launch classification. Not a giant
specification, final supporting-Page copy or implementation mechanics.

Show **one representative Home concept**, more developed than the optional free
teaser: proposed Header/Navigation, Hero/message, primary conversion, 2–3 representative
Sections and real proposed typography/palette/composition language. Do not produce
multiple alternatives, every Home Section, all supporting Pages or pixel controls.
Customers may approve, correct business misunderstandings, flag priorities, question
recommendations or reject inappropriate visual/business direction. They approve
the outcome rather than design the site.

Presence Direction is the concrete decision artifact for existing **Presence Plan
approval checkpoint #2**, not a fifth competing approval. Approved direction forms
the build basis; material subsequent changes need review. Then build the complete
presence, prepare final content/conversion/compositions and technical operation.
Final review focuses on factual accuracy, offers, important claims, contact/action
paths, omissions and overall appropriateness. Do not automatically reopen Visual
Direction unless materially wrong. Explicit approval of live-bound contact data,
prices, qualifications, guarantees, regulated claims, services, locations, policies
and other material facts is part of pre-launch checkpoint #3. Approval applies to
the reviewed revision, not later unseen changes, and does not replace readiness.

## Launch Readiness as a tracked workflow

Every applicable item should eventually retain what must be true, applicability/
priority, current state, accountable responsibility, dependencies/blocker, verification
evidence/environment/time and affected launch scope. This is a conceptual task/state
contract, not a schema or enterprise project-management system. Partly manual work
still needs an owner and recorded verification; automation is not the definition of ready.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not needed | Explicitly assessed as inapplicable, with reason. Not a way to hide missing work. |
| Needed | Applicable requirement identified; work or evidence remains outstanding. |
| In progress | Work/coordination underway; not ready merely because requested from a provider. |
| Ready | Preparation completed and available for verification in the relevant environment. |
| Verified | Evidence demonstrates the required behavior/approval for the stated scope/environment. Recheck if material state changes. |

Responsibility categories are **MioPages / Customer / External provider**, with an
identifiable accountable person/team where appropriate. MioPages coordinates the
overall launch; an external dependency must not become an unowned task. Keep customer
approvals distinct from technical verification. A previously verified item can
return to Needed/In progress when dependencies, facts, credentials or scope change.

invent a native form requirement. Observations remain distinct from completed calls,
qualified leads or customer outcomes. Pre-cutover checks use appropriate staging/

must not be falsely marked Verified before they can run.

### Launch priority and customer handoff

**Required before launch:** customer approval, working agreed conversion path and
lead delivery, authorized domain/DNS/SSL preparation, minimum measurement/reporting,
privacy/accessibility/security/resilience baseline and necessary indexing/continuity
correctness. Applicable pre-cutover requirements need verification; unresolved
critical blockers prevent authorized cutover, not merely appear in a later backlog.

**Conditional but required when applicable:** CRM, booking, ecommerce, essential
listing dependencies, replacement redirects and other integrations needed for the
agreed customer outcome. A useful optional profile is not automatically a blocker.

**May mature after launch:** richer reporting, advanced analytics automation and more
advanced monitoring/optimization. This does not defer basic first-party collection,
lead delivery, recovery or operational monitoring. GA4/Search Console are preferred
primary analytical sources in their respective domains when usable; unavailability
must trigger explicit fallback/limited reporting, not block operation. First-party
lead/contact signals and customer outcome feedback remain important while Google works.
A known zero-traffic baseline is valid, missing independent fallback collection is
not. Initial reports may be simple/operator-assisted but need real
evidence and explicit uncertainty.

After cutover, perform live checks promptly under an owned plan and use the defined
correction/rollback path if critical behavior fails. Go-live approval authorizes
the agreed launch only when readiness/operational gates are satisfied. Record actual
go-live separately from planned date for baseline and Ongoing billing confirmation.

The customer should know how to report business changes, approve recommendations,
ask for help, receive reports, what MioPages monitors, normal Ongoing scope and what
may become Campaign/major Revamp/exceptional work. This is continuing managed care,
not a traditional “here are the keys” handoff. Existing content and opportunity loops
govern ongoing preparation, approval, measurement and learning.

## Readiness model versus operational runbooks

The platform readiness model defines **what must be true, who owns it, state and
verification**. Runbooks define **how to perform provider-specific procedures**.
Future runbooks may cover 20i domain registration, external-domain connection, DNS
changes/email-record preservation, GA4/GSC setup, SMTP testing, replacement cutover
and DNS rollback. Do not bind core architecture to 20i or equate a written procedure
with completed customer-specific verification. No runbook automation is built here.

## Existing gates and C0 proving

Reuse P11/P13/P14 for prospect/intake reuse, Review synthesis/operator approval and
controlled delivery; P1/P2/P3/P7 for authorization, confirmed writes, reviewed
direction and approval provenance; P26 for commitment/payment/exit terms; P5/P6 for
export/ownership; P12/P24/P25 for the preferred-primary/fallback measurement hierarchy;
P9/P10/P16 for serving/URL/replacement continuity; P17/A1–A3 for media where applicable;
P18–P20 for operation, readiness evidence, rollback/recovery and tenant safety; P21
for supported direction; and C3/C7 for actual content review/publication. Bounded
retention remains P4. No new gate IDs or automatic closure of existing gates.

C0 should test shared intake reuse, operator Review effort, optional teaser value,
commercial clarity, targeted questions, one direction/Home concept, readiness
ownership/evidence, lead handoff, controlled cutover and Ongoing continuity. These
are proving goals, not implemented workflows, numerical service entitlements or
permission to launch. Free Review implementation remains a later authorized slice.
