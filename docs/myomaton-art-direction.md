# Myomaton art direction

## Decision and scope

Target: **Ooni confidence + Twillory restraint + a restrained amount of Welly
playfulness**. Technical, human, curious, credible and non-hyped: serious fun.
Confidence comes from clear claims and real work; personality comes from editorial
contrast and timing, not novelty effects. Avoid README/documentation presentation,
generic SaaS, corporate blue templates, toy-brand exuberance and maker-site clutter.

The reusable reference-v2 composition system is implemented and has received
human visual acceptance for this checkpoint. This brief records the accepted
Myomaton proving preview, not a customer-state transition. References influence
traits, not layouts, artwork, wording or branded motifs to copy.

## Eleven implementation-guiding traits

1. **Deliberate first impression.** Hero display should be visibly larger than
   ordinary Section headings, with a short measure (roughly 12–18 characters per
   line for a proposition), deliberate space above/below and one clear next step.
   Do not force a short Page title into artificial line breaks.
2. **Contrast through roles.** Use display, statement, Section and item scales;
   readable body stays stable. A statement may approach display scale while
   retaining h2 semantics. Do not enlarge every heading to simulate confidence.
3. **Quiet explanation.** Multi-paragraph Intro copy uses reading width, left
   alignment and normal spacing; it should reward reading without dominating the
   Page. Airy does not mean every Section receives identical oversized padding.
4. **Rhythm by purpose.** Organize attention as arrival → explanation → discovery
   or evidence → one possible statement pause → next step. Use surfaces to mark
   purposeful groups, never odd/even Section index as the design rationale.
5. **One memorable editorial pause.** A concise, true statement with generous
   space may interrupt a long Page once. It is neither a second Hero nor a sales
   banner. Long explanations must remain available rather than being clipped.
6. **Equal peers, distinct groups.** Collections share item scale, spacing and
   surface. Projects and principles gain identity from their content and group
   context; order never makes the first card larger, brighter or more important.
7. **Selective asymmetry.** Use existing text-first/image-first Intro splits to
   balance real photography against copy. Text-only Sections remain complete.
   Keep mobile heading → image → body → Action order.
8. **Purposeful two-accent behavior.** Primary green locates action and navigation
   emphasis; a reviewed blue secondary may punctuate editorial personality.
   The neutral canvas remains dominant; color never invents rank or business facts.
9. **Honest image moments.** Give available prototype photographs enough room to
   communicate physical work. Preserve natural fit, clean edges and negative space;
   do not distribute thumbnails or empty media slots to satisfy a template.
10. **Conversion without pressure.** Opening Action supports discovery; ending
    Action follows understanding. Proof precedes a higher-commitment invitation.
    Preserve the polished Navigation; no repeated button wall or sticky sales bar.
11. **Sparse life.** One legible Hero entrance and brief interaction feedback;
    optional later emphasis only under policy. Decoration is stationary and scarce.
    Motion must feel intentional and must never gate access to content.

## Bounded statement moment

The implemented finite Intro treatment is explicit: `treatment: "statement"`.
It retains h2 and plain copy. Heading bounds are 120 characters / 18 words;
compact support permits 360 characters / two paragraphs. Explicit statement-break
allows 900 characters / five paragraphs with separate reading-scale support.
Overlong or image-backed content safely falls back to ordinary Intro presentation.
No statement is inferred from ordering or short copy. Optional secondary wash is
readability-paired and policy-controlled. Existing explicit presentation wins.

The accepted disposable preview selects "Reality is an excellent design review"
on Home/About and "Ownership matters" on Principles. Full supporting content is
preserved. Projects has no statement. These are deliberate customer-preview
selections, not automatic profile behavior or applied customer configuration.

## Hero art direction and motion recipe

A wide asymmetric text-led field replaces the placeholder opening. Heading and
support occupy distinct but aligned regions, with an existing primary Action below
support. There is no media Hero, forced viewport height, invented proposition,
artificial title break or decorative bar. Full content remains available.

The accepted shared recipe uses named eyebrow/heading/support/Action parts:
heading starts immediately, rises 96px desktop / 56px narrow over 1150ms with
cubic-bezier(.22,.6,.35,1). Opacity starts at .35 and reaches .9 early, making
travel readable without an empty opening. Support rises 24px over 650ms from
100ms; Action rises 18px over 450ms from 260ms; supplied eyebrow uses 8px/360ms.
Only opacity/transform animate. CSS establishes final geometry from first render,
requires no scroll observer and completes without JavaScript. Reduced motion and
focus render final state immediately. The whole Hero counts as one Page event.
See the [runtime contract](visual-direction.md#staged-hero-and-page-motion-contract).

## Product motion language

Initial-load Hero is the main automatic event. Minimal spends its single reservation
there; it does not reveal every Section. Light may allow up to two additional
whole-Section emphasis events, but start with zero for Myomaton. Existing allocator
only admits populated Hero/CTA; do not imply statement/collection reveals exist.

If later justified, one closing CTA may reveal once after entering the viewport,
with 12–16px travel over 400–500ms, opacity .35 → 1. Never animate peer cards
individually, never rearm on scrolling back, and never run simultaneous automatic
sequences. The current non-Hero observer does not itself serialize simultaneous
entries; preserve the ceiling and refine coordination before relying on Light.

Interaction feedback: 120–180ms color/surface emphasis on hover; immediate visible
focus, with no delayed outline or position movement. Menu state must remain clear
instantly; a future 160–220ms panel transition is optional, not a navigation redesign.
Reduced motion removes nonessential interpolation. No mandatory route transitions,
image zooms, parallax, ambient motion or animation dependency/package is required.

## Two accents and a small decorative vocabulary

Retain primary green for conversion and current-navigation emphasis; links retain
underlines and current location retains non-color cues. The accepted disposable palette uses blue as secondary personality accent;
these reviewed values remain preview-only and are not stored in real customer state. Avoid the generic all-blue SaaS palette.

Primary/secondary are roles, not alternating Section paints. Most Page area stays
warm neutral. Allow one editorial secondary moment; do not color every other row, assign different hues to peers or use secondary
blue for a rival primary Action. Washes are derived platform recipes with readable
foregrounds and opaque fallback, never arbitrary per-Section color inputs.
Maintain at least the current 4.5:1 text-pairing floor and visible focus/control
boundaries; do not assume a decorative text pairing also proves control contrast.

Current vocabulary: **semantic color fields and quiet surface wash**. Keep both static,
section-level and tied to meaningful editorial composition. The implicit wash is
policy-controlled; explicit surfaces remain semantic choices. There is no automatic
Hero ornament allocator. Use secondary moments sparingly, not as filler.
Geometric shapes, gradients and layered panels are unnecessary for the first slice.
No first-item motif, feature rank, arbitrary canvas or customer-uploaded executable
decoration. The wash should be a named semantic surface recipe, not another palette
editor. Secondary color roles and wash consumption are implemented in the reusable
contract. Customer palette selection remains separate from the runtime.

## Images and mobile Navigation

With many useful images, distribute a few large purpose-led moments through
existing Intro splits/stack; a real Gallery remains a separately planned capability.
With few, reserve images for the strongest evidence instead of filling every
Section. With none, typography, statement rhythm, surfaces and equal collections
must produce a complete Page. No empty media placeholders or generated substitute
evidence. Product photographs show prototype reality, not proof of unsupported
performance. Retain Asset identity, usage-specific alt semantics, natural fit and
clean presentation; separation comes from layout and whitespace rather than rules.

Keep improved mobile Navigation: polished non-modal panel, deliberate padding,
48px link targets, clear Menu/Close and current-Page state, restrained shadow and
opaque readable backdrop. Preserve Enter/Space, Arrow Down to first link, Escape
and selection focus return, outside-pointer dismissal and ordinary Tab order.
No modal trap, added overlay, new breakpoint or repeated redesign is justified.
Desktop keeps the same shallow server-rendered hierarchy.

## Commercial rhythm and pre-transition Page mapping

For a lead-generation client: establish relevance → explain the offer → enable
discovery → show genuine evidence → make the next step easy. One primary Action
at arrival and one after a meaningful evidence/explanation group or at the close
is a starting cadence, not a quota. Internal progression can use text links or
existing Actions; optional Header Contact emphasis depends on real configured
facts/destination. A Contact Section should reduce uncertainty with clear fields,
help and submission feedback. Do not invent testimonials, credentials, contact
details, service promises or proof to fill a visual role.

Myomaton's current goal is discovery/following the work, not purchasing robots or
submitting a sales inquiry. There is no Contact Definition; do not add one as part
of visual implementation. The five existing Actions and Navigation remain authority.

## External-reference onboarding model

See the durable planned [intake contract](onboarding-optimization.md#external-reference-intake).
Curated platform examples demonstrate actual reproducible combinations. External
competitor or unrelated sites communicate preferences and may exceed supported
capabilities. They must never be presented as selectable templates.

Initial operator-assisted record: URL/type/date/context; liked/disliked traits;
priority and rationale; observed versus inferred traits; proposed supported
profile/preferences/token roles; gaps; preview/review decision. AI may suggest
trait extraction through the provider-neutral boundary; customer/operator review
establishes intent. Unknown or conflicting preferences return for clarification.
This is a planned workflow, not a schema, importer, writer or implemented UI.

For Myomaton, confidence is highest priority, restraint constrains density/effects,
playfulness is a sparse accent. No reference layout, copy or branded asset is an
implementation input. Unsupported traits are reported as gaps, not approximated
through customer-specific CSS.
