# Design brief for C0's redesign (approved by the Principal, Nov 27)

What Core is asked to do in one clean pass. Each line comes from the Principal's own words, or is
marked as a proposal for the Principal to change.

## Keep (don't redo)
- **The words**, exactly: all ten pages' text, already approved and unchanged since Nov 26.
- **The Report, the strategy, the 10-page Site plan and the footer.**
- **The logo.**

## Change
- **A clearly different look.** "Still the same damned design" (Nov 27): the new design must not
  look like the earlier MioPages site (the indigo opening band, and every section in its own
  rounded box). Core chooses the new look from its research: "contemporary, appropriate, and
  research infused" (Nov 27).
- **Not bloated.** "A natural limit to what a visitor can comprehend on a page" (Nov 26). Each
  page reads as a clear sequence, not a wall of panels.
- **Corners.** "Are the strong rounded edges too extreme?" (Nov 27) Core decides, guided by its
  research; nothing extreme.
- **Nothing lies over anything else.** Pictures and panels sit beside or between text, never
  across it.
- **Buttons go somewhere.** "Free Review" and "Direct Start" go to those pages until the service
  forms exist.

## How much goes on a page

Driven by need: "neither extravagant nor too constricted" (the Principal, Nov 27). For this pass
the numbers below are a rough ceiling, never a target; use fewer wherever the page needs fewer.
- **Pictures:** one main picture per page, plus at most two more where they show something the
  words can't. Today it's 7–18 per page.
- **Diagrams and charts:** only where the page explains a process or a comparison; at most one
  per page. Today about 40 of the frames ask for one.
- **Boxes (bordered panels):** only to group things that really are parallel (for example
  "You provide" and "MioPages handles"); no boxes inside boxes. Today it's 15–36 per page.
- **Pull quotes:** none, unless one genuinely helps a long page.

## Cost and checking
- One Core pass: the design, then the ten page layouts with the words unchanged. About **$3.50**
  in development; a real Client's first build costs about the same. The run has $2.20 left, so
  the cap needs to rise to about **$21**.
- Every page is checked on miopages.org (desktop and phone, the design rules, nothing overlapping)
  before the Principal sees it. Pictures for any new frames come after the Principal likes the
  layout.
