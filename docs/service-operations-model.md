# Launch and Ongoing service operations

## Continuous relationship, distinct service entities

**Launch establishes the presence. Ongoing earns the relationship.** They are
separate service entities and commercial responsibilities designed as one
continuous operating model, not a website project followed by an unrelated retainer.
These are service concepts, not new database entities defined by this document.

Launch establishes trusted business knowledge, structured customer state, effective
public presentation, conversion/lead paths, measurement foundations and an initial
optimization/content-growth backlog. Ongoing observes, reports, recommends, gathers
knowledge, improves content and conversion paths, and measures again through
controlled system/operator/customer intervention.

The [service model](web-presence-service-model.md) owns reusable customer outcomes;
this document owns Launch scope, handoff, cadence, responsibility and economics.
The [growth loop](content-growth-loop.md) and
[measurement architecture](measurement-and-analytics.md) retain their detailed
contracts. This is planned operating direction unless expressly identified as
implemented; it adds no runtime, staffing system, service guarantee or customer state.

## Standard Launch

Standard Launch is the normal good-fit customer envelope. Its typical scope is:

- Structured onboarding and trusted business-knowledge capture.
- Current-presence and external-profile inventory, existing systems and constraints.
- Core services/products/offers, audiences, customer needs, differentiators and
  proof where available; do not invent credentials or testimonials.
- A core Managed Site with essential Pages/Navigation, an appropriate Contact/lead
  path, bounded Visual Direction and initial content foundation.
- First-party measurement baseline; preferred optional GA4/Search Console setup or
  support where practical, authorized and proven. Their absence does not block the core.
- Domain/hosting/deployment coordination where applicable, customer review/approval,
  go-live verification and direct transition into Ongoing.

The customer supplies business truth, goals, preferences, Assets, existing systems
and useful examples when available. They do not need to supply a sitemap,
wireframes, a formal design brief or pixel-level direction. The system/operator
interprets that input into supported structure and presentation for review.
Customers approve outcomes rather than become webmasters.

This envelope is product intent, not a statement that every business entity,
integration or production workflow already exists. Use supported structured
content; general Offering/Testimonial domains and native publishing retain their
status in the [capability catalog](platform-capabilities.md). Do not sell an unbuilt
feature or compensate with a bespoke component.

### Internal complexity guidance

Typical Standard Launch may involve one primary business/brand, one location or
simple service area, roughly 5–10 core services/offers, a small team where relevant,
modest existing content, one primary lead/conversion path, a normal domain/hosting
transition, one or two straightforward supported integrations and normal analytics/
search setup.

These are internal scoping signals, not public guarantees, schema caps or immutable
limits. Assess actual content, approval, operational and continuity burden. A small
catalog can still have complex requirements; additional Pages alone do not establish
Expanded Launch. Agree the supported scope before establishment work begins.

### Launch-to-Ongoing handoff

The handoff should make trusted facts, approved presentation, conversion paths,
relevant account/domain responsibility, operational ownership and measurement
availability reviewable. Verify supported routes/resources and the configured lead
path; record unresolved limitations rather than hide them as completed work.
Production go-live remains subject to existing security, Contact, routing,
discovery, storage and recovery gates.

Carry the measurement baseline, confirmed priorities and small initial opportunity
backlog directly into Ongoing. Record known versus unavailable signals, pending
customer answers and attention ownership. Publication approval is specific to the
reviewed outcome; Launch approval is not blanket permission for later factual edits
or AI-content publication. Handoff is a continuation of knowledge and responsibility,
not a restart of discovery.

## Expanded Launch

Expanded Launch handles more supported complexity, not bespoke development.
Potential triggers include multiple locations, larger catalogs, large team/resource
collections, a larger approval surface, multiple supported integrations, multiple
brands/business units where supported, meaningful redirect/SEO preservation,
substantial existing search/analytics continuity, or substantial content transfer
where a supported migration path exists.

Use the same structured platform, supported entity types, bounded composition/
design system and supported integration patterns. More time can be needed for
inventory, supported configuration, validation, approvals and continuity planning.
Extra scope cannot make an unsupported domain, integration or migration magically
available. Existing-site coexistence is not automatic ingestion/reconstruction;
content transfer is an explicitly reviewed capability when supported, not a promise
to clone arbitrary sites or layouts.

## Complexity and customization

**Complexity may scale. Customization does not.** Bespoke work damages service
economics because it cannot be staffed or priced repeatably. If normal customers
need bespoke engineering to succeed, the product premise has failed: revisit fit,
boundaries or a genuinely reusable capability rather than normalize exceptions.

Reject customer-specific CSS, arbitrary layouts, one-off components, custom
applications disguised as Web Presence work, unlimited design revisions and
arbitrary page-builder control. Bounded choices can still produce strong authored
design. Professional quality is essential, but exhaustive pixel control is outside
fit. A request may trigger product development; the customer does not automatically
buy bespoke development.

## Supported external integrations

Integrate specialized external capability rather than recreate it as customer
software. Potential examples include restaurant ordering/delivery, scheduling/
booking, CRM handoff, donations, event registration, ecommerce checkout, external
customer portals and application/intake systems. These are categories to evaluate,
not a list of currently implemented connectors or native transactional features.

A supported integration uses stable public methods, reusable patterns, acceptable
security, maintainable ownership/failure boundaries, bounded operator burden and
reasonable handoff measurement where possible. A public destination link can be
appropriate when existing Action semantics support it; a link is not a verified
transaction, booking or automated data exchange. Keep specialized operational
behavior in the external service and preserve a coherent customer experience.

### Decision framework

For an unsupported service request, assess reuse across customers, broader market
value, fit as a core capability or optional integration, bounded implementation,
ongoing administration/reporting, provider stability/support, coherent experience
and measurable conversion handoff. Record a classification:

| Classification | Decision boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Core capability | Recurring product need belongs in shared bounded platform development, subject to the existing capability/gate review. |
| Supported optional integration | Repeatable adapter/configuration with a maintainable scope and service burden. Availability begins after implementation/validation, not at classification. |
| Future roadmap | Potential value, but not a current customer commitment. |
| Partner/referral | Another provider is better placed to deliver the requirement; clarify ownership and handoff. |
| Outside scope | Incompatible, insecure, unstable or uneconomic requirement; do not disguise it as Expanded Launch. |

No customer-only integration by default. Earlier “custom integration” wording for
Adjunct/coexistence means reviewed supported configuration and coordination, not
a permission for bespoke software. The [service provider boundary](web-presence-service-model.md#20i-and-infrastructure-boundary)
keeps infrastructure, including provisional 20i use, separate from product identity.

### Integration charging principle

A supported optional integration may carry a fixed Launch increment, a fixed
Ongoing increment for recurring support/reporting, or no extra charge when burden
is negligible. Charging should follow repeatable service burden, not open-ended
custom estimates. No price, fixed entitlement or final commercial term is set here.

## Progressive onboarding and selective customer effort

The system can work with limited information, but accurate business knowledge
materially improves the attainable outcome. Missing information stays unknown;
limited input never authorizes plausible invented facts or unsupported claims.
Avoid exhaustive labor-intensive discovery as the normal service model.

| Level | Intended operation |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum viable onboarding | Gather enough trusted identity, offering/audience, goals, Assets and valid Contact/next-step information for a credible initial presence. Confirm essential facts and unresolved limits. |
| Progressive enrichment | Ask the highest-value missing questions when relevant to content, business changes or a recommendation. Reuse approved knowledge rather than repeat intake. |
| Operator-assisted exception | Additional help when a customer genuinely needs it, with scope/time visible; not the default assumption of a large discovery engagement. |

Initial proving can use operator-assisted intake before self-service exists. That
implementation bridge does not define the target workload or justify routine
manual reconstruction. The [onboarding model](onboarding-optimization.md) owns
trust, conditional questions and authorized canonical writes.

**The system should do work continuously; the customer should contribute
selectively.** Ask for facts or judgment the system cannot reasonably know: “Has
this service changed?”, “Which of these three topics matters most?”, “Answer these
three questions so we can draft,” or “Did these leads become customers?” Do not
require customers to maintain another complex dashboard or marketing workflow.

## Operating cadences

Cadences describe intended service behavior as capabilities become available;
they are not implemented schedulers, publication quotas or contractual response times.

| Cadence | Intended value cycle |
| --- | --- |
| Continuous | Observe operation/uptime, first-party traffic and Actions, available referrals, lead/Contact activity, content performance, optimization signals, stale/missing information and opportunity candidates. No routine customer action required. |
| Monthly | Concise account of what happened, leads/Contact activity, known/confirmed outcomes, improvements and attention needs; 1–3 prioritized recommendations, draft/review items and targeted questions where needed. No analytics dump or artificial quota when evidence is insufficient. |
| Quarterly | Broader view of service/topic performance, accumulated content effects, useful channels, important underperforming Pages, business/positioning priorities, external-presence accuracy and next-period recommendations. A meeting is optional when it adds value. |
| Triggered | Respond to meaningful traffic/lead-volume changes, emerging search visibility, high activity with weak conversion, recurring questions, new business/service facts, seasonal opportunities, stale critical information or sufficiently supported content opportunities. Do not wait for a calendar cycle when attention is useful sooner. |

Calibrate triggers to available evidence and customer context; sparse data should
not cause confident anomaly claims or noisy prompts. Triggered action may mean
review, a customer question or an operational response, not automatic publication
or changes to protected business facts. Missing Google data should be disclosed
without interrupting independent first-party reporting. Technical-health and
business-conversion signals remain separate under the measurement contract.

## Value and conversion reporting

Use plain language: what happened, what generated useful activity, what became a
lead, what became a customer where known, what improved and what should happen
next. Link recommendations to evidence and purpose; show unknowns and the observation
period. Customers should not have to interpret raw analytics. Do not invent ROI,
equate publication volume with value or claim every improvement caused a sale.

The existing [conversion ladder](measurement-and-analytics.md#conversion-ladder-and-business-value-reports)
remains authoritative: Observed means a directly measured visitor/Action/form/Contact
event; Attributed is a reasonable source/campaign/channel association; Customer
confirmed is reported business value; Unknown means the downstream result is not
reported. These levels do not establish full-funnel attribution.

### First-party lead signals and outcome confirmation

Lead/Contact conversion is a first-class planned concept for the initial ICP.
Direct signals include accepted Contact submissions, click-to-call, click-to-email,
and explicitly designated quote/request, booking/request or other lead Actions.
A request Action click does not prove a completed booking or qualified lead. Track
those distinctions rather than display every click as a new inquiry. Existing
Action destinations are not changed or invented for measurement purposes.

Make downstream confirmation easy without requiring a CRM. When actual evidence
supports it, a prompt might say: “Five leads came through MioPages this month.
What happened?” For each known lead, offer **Became customer**, **Still open**,
**Did not convert**, or **Not sure**. Do not attach these choices to anonymous
phone/email clicks as if identifiable leads had been captured. Nonresponse remains
Unknown; customer confirmation should be minimal, attributable and correctable
under appropriate access/privacy/retention rules.

This is a planned lightweight workflow, not an implemented CRM or outcome table.
Future CRM integration may improve it automatically without becoming a prerequisite.
The [growth loop](content-growth-loop.md) owns grounded content work and review;
the [measurement architecture](measurement-and-analytics.md) owns event meaning,
normalization, attribution and provider failure.

## Responsibility layers

**Automate observation and preparation. Human-review judgment. Customer-approve
business truth.** The layers compose; system preparation is not publication approval.

| Layer | Intended work |
| --- | --- |
| System-automatic | Authorized measurement collection, source classification, lead-event tracking, content monitoring, opportunity scoring, report assembly, trusted-knowledge drafts, stale-information reminders and anomaly detection. Preserve uncertainty; these automations are planned, not current implementations. |
| Operator-reviewed | Prioritize recommendations, review ambiguity and draft quality, resolve conflicting facts with the customer, handle supported infrastructure/content-transfer exceptions, and assess material trust/positioning decisions. Operator review cannot manufacture factual authority. |
| Customer-approved | Final business claims, material service/pricing/availability changes, externally published AI-assisted content, major positioning/brand changes and downstream outcomes only the customer knows. Approval remains scoped to an actual reviewed outcome. |

The [growth approval contract](content-growth-loop.md#human-review-and-publication-approval)
permits an authorized customer/operator reviewer for initial publication; customer
business authority governs factual/material decisions, including authorized delegated
approval where explicitly established. Do not infer delegation from an account
assignment or an admin role. Broad automation policy cannot override protected facts,
pricing approval or initial human publication review. AI has no independent responsibility.

## Operator and team model

The initial service may have one operator. Single-operator operation is the simplest
case of multi-operator operation, not an architecture in which one identity always
does everything. A lightweight future model should support operator identity, a
finite role, customer/account assignment, allowed action categories, attention-item
ownership and bounded audit/provenance. No workforce-management system is proposed.

| Provisional role | Scope concept |
| --- | --- |
| Owner / Admin | Full service administration within enforced tenant/security and customer-approval boundaries; not permission to bypass business authority. |
| Content Reviewer | Recommendations, drafts and publishing-side review within assigned scope. |
| Technical Operator | Hosting, deployment, supported integrations and operational issues. |
| Account / Success | Customer prompts, follow-up and outcome-reporting coordination. |
| Analyst / Viewer | Authorized measurement/report visibility; read-only by default. |

These are provisional role concepts, not implemented permissions or required launch
UI. One operator can simulate several roles during testing; actual caller identity
and authorized scope must still be clear. Production authentication, least privilege
and tenant isolation remain P7/P19 obligations even before a team exists. See
[management security](security-operations.md#management-access-and-tenant-isolation).

As customer count grows, specialization and role-based attention routing can increase
without changing the customer model. Preserve a consistent customer experience;
do not require customers to navigate the internal team structure.

### Exception-driven attention queue

Operator work should be driven by attention needs, not a checklist requiring routine
inspection of every account. Conceptual account/item attention states are **no
action**, **customer prompt**, **operator review**, and **urgent operator attention**.
No action need not create a task. Distinguish attention class/severity from workflow
status; these are concepts, not prescribed enums or existing dashboard states.

An item may eventually carry customer, type/severity, trigger/evidence, recommended
action, assigned operator/role, status, due/context and bounded audit history. Group
duplicates and give each actionable item a clear owner; escalation should not silently
become everyone's responsibility. Customer questions and operational issues need
appropriate routing without exposing private facts to unrelated operators. Urgency
does not bypass approval. Response commitments remain future service-policy decisions.

## Service economics and planning hypotheses

Track Launch operator hours, **service minutes per customer per month**, infrastructure
cost, AI/API usage, exceptional support time, incidents and content/review activity.
Future acquisition cost can supplement these. Service minutes are the strongest
operating workload metric: human time should concentrate where judgment adds value,
not where software can reliably do the work. Capture exceptional time as well as
routine time rather than hide it from averages.

| Non-canonical planning target | Current hypothesis to test |
| --- | --- |
| Normal / Standard Launch | Approximately 3–6 operator hours. |
| Expanded Launch | Approximately 6–10 operator hours. |
| Normal Ongoing | Ideally under 45 operator minutes/customer/month. |
| More complex Ongoing | Ideally under 60 operator minutes/customer/month. |

These ranges are internal planning assumptions, not contractual promises, platform
invariants, staffing commitments or permission to skip necessary review. Measure
actual workload and outcomes. Persistent overruns may indicate wrong service
boundaries, insufficient automation, unsuitable pricing or poor customer fit;
investigate those causes instead of relying on unpaid bespoke effort. D6 already
owns the durable economics principle; no actual prices are established here.

## Commercial structure, retention and fit

Current non-final direction is a fixed/bounded Launch component and a recurring
Ongoing component. A likely pattern is Standard Launch, Expanded Launch, one primary
Ongoing service and bounded optional integrations/value-adds. Avoid a maze of
packages; scope and recurring burden should remain comprehensible. Pricing, terms,
service levels and final packaging need separate commercial validation.

Ongoing's value is that the system keeps helping the presence improve, not primarily
payment to prevent a website from breaking. Reliable operation matters, alongside
recommendations, content, optimization, business updates, conversion refinement and
demonstrated progress. Keep an understandable exit path; lock-in is not retention.

Good fit: small organizations with real expertise/business knowledge, limited desire
or capacity for web management, willingness to use bounded design/integration choices
and interest in measurable improvement. Poor fit: arbitrary HTML/CSS, exhaustive pixel
control, continuous bespoke redesign, custom applications, customer-only integrations
without a reusable support path, or full CRM/marketing-automation replacement at launch.
The [MioPages ICP](miopages-c0-product-model.md#initial-customer-hypothesis) is a proving
hypothesis, not an industry or head-count invariant.

## MioPages C0 proving work

Use MioPages to test Standard Launch scope/time assumptions, progressive onboarding,
attention routing, value reports, the conversion ladder, easy outcome confirmation,
operator-role simulation, service-minute tracking, bounded integration decisions
and direct Launch-to-Ongoing continuity. Record gaps and revise hypotheses based on
observed burden/value, not a handcrafted showcase exempt from the platform rules.
MioPages-specific account/provider/content choices do not become platform defaults.

## Implementation status and related contracts

- **Implemented now:** structured customer-state/rendering foundations, bounded
  Visual Direction, Actions/Navigation, managed Assets and development Contact
  foundation as cataloged in [platform capabilities](platform-capabilities.md).
  These do not implement the operating cadences or general service management.
- **Planned core / pre-launch:** repeatable supported Launch/handoff, progressive
  intake, production operational readiness, independent measurement, concise value
  reporting, lead/outcome confirmation, grounded draft/review/publish, a small
  attention workflow and service-time observability. Minimum authorized operator
  access is required before production management; broad team tooling is not.
- **Future:** richer team administration/routing, additional supported integrations,
  CRM/provider outcome automation, automated distribution and deeper attribution.
  Role concepts and provisional pricing increments are not implemented products.
