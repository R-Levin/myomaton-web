# Launch and Ongoing service operations

The [customer journey and change-management contract](customer-journey-and-change-management.md)
owns the gated Review, free/paid handoff, four approval checkpoints and ongoing
business-change process. This document continues to own service scope, cadence,
responsibilities and operator-time hypotheses; planning targets are not SLAs.

## Continuous relationship, distinct service entities

**Launch establishes the presence. Ongoing earns the relationship.** They are
separate service entities and commercial responsibilities designed as one
continuous operating model, not a website project followed by an unrelated retainer.
These are service concepts, not new database entities defined by this document.

Launch establishes trusted business knowledge, structured customer state, effective
public presentation, conversion/lead paths, measurement foundations and an initial
optimization/content-growth backlog. Ongoing observes, reports, recommends, gathers
knowledge, improves content and conversion paths, and measures again through
controlled system/operator/customer intervention.

The [service model](web-presence-service-model.md) owns reusable customer outcomes;
this document owns Launch scope, handoff, cadence, responsibility and economics.
The [growth loop](content-growth-loop.md) and
[measurement architecture](measurement-and-analytics.md) retain their detailed
contracts. This is planned operating direction unless expressly identified as
implemented; it adds no runtime, staffing system, service guarantee or customer state.

## Standard Launch

Most businesses/organizations should fit Standard. Absorb normal customer-to-customer
effort variation and minor complexity; Standard is not a teaser. The
[progressive onboarding/readiness model](progressive-onboarding-and-launch-readiness.md)
owns shared intake, Presence Direction and verified launch handoff. Its working
MioPages prices/timing are business-specific assumptions pending final review,
not universal service entitlements or canonical pricing updates.

Standard Launch is the normal good-fit customer envelope. Its typical scope is:

- Full structured Commercial V1 interview and trusted business-knowledge capture,
  reusing existing intake/Review knowledge with targeted gap follow-up.
- Current-presence and external-profile inventory, existing systems and constraints.
- Core services/products/offers, audiences, customer needs, differentiators and
  proof where available; do not invent credentials or testimonials.
- A core Managed Site with essential Pages/Navigation, an appropriate Contact/lead
  path, bounded Visual Direction and initial content foundation.
- First-party MioPages measurement baseline, important Action and Contact
  acceptance/delivery evidence, useful trends and concise Performance Snapshot.
  GA4/Search Console are optional later enrichment, outside the V1 critical path.
- Domain/hosting/deployment coordination where applicable, customer review/approval,
  go-live verification and direct transition into Ongoing.

The customer supplies business truth, goals, preferences, Assets, existing systems
and useful examples when available. They do not need to supply a sitemap,
wireframes, a formal design brief or pixel-level direction. The system uses practical AI assistance to orchestrate existing multi-page
generation into a near-finished candidate for operator QA/correction and customer
approval. Operators resolve exceptions and refine rather than routinely assemble
each Page. Measure actual operator time/cost-to-serve from future operations.
Customers approve outcomes rather than become webmasters.

This envelope is product intent, not a statement that every business entity,
integration or production workflow already exists. Use supported structured
content; general Offering/Testimonial domains and native publishing retain their
status in the [capability catalog](platform-capabilities.md). Do not sell an unbuilt
feature or compensate with a bespoke component.

### Internal complexity guidance

Typical Standard Launch may involve one primary business/brand, one location or
simple service area, roughly 5–10 core services/offers, a small team where relevant,
modest existing content, one primary lead/conversion path, a normal domain/hosting
transition, one or two straightforward supported integrations and normal analytics/
search setup.

These are internal scoping signals, not public guarantees, schema caps or immutable
limits. Assess actual content, approval, operational and continuity burden. A small
catalog can still have complex requirements; additional Pages alone do not establish
Expanded Launch. Agree the supported scope before establishment work begins.

### Launch-to-Ongoing handoff

The handoff should make trusted facts, approved presentation, conversion paths,
relevant account/domain responsibility, operational ownership and measurement
availability reviewable. Verify supported routes/resources and the configured lead
path; record unresolved limitations rather than hide them as completed work.
Production go-live remains subject to existing security, Contact, routing,
discovery, storage and recovery gates.

Carry the measurement baseline, confirmed priorities and small initial opportunity
backlog directly into Ongoing. Record known versus unavailable signals, pending
customer answers and attention ownership. Publication approval is specific to the
reviewed outcome; Launch approval is not blanket permission for later factual edits
or AI-content publication. Handoff is a continuation of knowledge and responsibility,
not a restart of discovery.

## Expanded Launch

Expanded Launch is a rare exception for materially unusual supported complexity,
not bespoke development or a default upgrade for normal variation. A very large
catalog, many locations or substantial unusual supported setup/integration/migration
burden may justify it when economics/operating effort materially change. Additional
Pages, a minor integration or ordinary location/content variation alone must not
trigger added charges. Operator/customer confirm exceptional scope and price before
commitment; Campaigns/major Revamps are separately scoped only when justified.

Use the same structured platform, supported entity types, bounded composition/
design system and supported integration patterns. More time can be needed for
inventory, supported configuration, validation, approvals and continuity planning.
Extra scope cannot make an unsupported domain, integration or migration magically
available. Existing-site coexistence is not automatic ingestion/reconstruction;
content transfer is an explicitly reviewed capability when supported, not a promise
to clone arbitrary sites or layouts.

## Complexity and customization

**Complexity may scale. Customization does not.** Bespoke work damages service
economics because it cannot be staffed or priced repeatably. If normal customers
need bespoke engineering to succeed, the product premise has failed: revisit fit,
boundaries or a genuinely reusable capability rather than normalize exceptions.

Reject customer-specific CSS, arbitrary layouts, one-off components, custom
applications disguised as Web Presence work, unlimited design revisions and
arbitrary page-builder control. Bounded choices can still produce strong authored
design. Professional quality is essential, but exhaustive pixel control is outside
fit. A request may trigger product development; the customer does not automatically
buy bespoke development.

## Supported external integrations

Integrate specialized external capability rather than recreate it as customer
software. Potential examples include restaurant ordering/delivery, scheduling/
booking, CRM handoff, donations, event registration, ecommerce checkout, external
customer portals and application/intake systems. These are categories to evaluate,
not a list of currently implemented connectors or native transactional features.

A supported integration uses stable public methods, reusable patterns, acceptable
security, maintainable ownership/failure boundaries, bounded operator burden and
reasonable handoff measurement where possible. A public destination link can be
appropriate when existing Action semantics support it; a link is not a verified
transaction, booking or automated data exchange. Keep specialized operational
behavior in the external service and preserve a coherent customer experience.

### Decision framework

For an unsupported service request, assess reuse across customers, broader market
value, fit as a core capability or optional integration, bounded implementation,
ongoing administration/reporting, provider stability/support, coherent experience
and measurable conversion handoff. Record a classification:

| Classification | Decision boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Core capability | Recurring product need belongs in shared bounded platform development, subject to the existing capability/gate review. |
| Supported optional integration | Repeatable adapter/configuration with a maintainable scope and service burden. Availability begins after implementation/validation, not at classification. |
| Future roadmap | Potential value, but not a current customer commitment. |
| Partner/referral | Another provider is better placed to deliver the requirement; clarify ownership and handoff. |
| Outside scope | Incompatible, insecure, unstable or uneconomic requirement; do not disguise it as Expanded Launch. |

No customer-only integration by default. Earlier “custom integration” wording for
Adjunct/coexistence means reviewed supported configuration and coordination, not
a permission for bespoke software. The [service provider boundary](web-presence-service-model.md#20i-and-infrastructure-boundary)
keeps infrastructure, including provisional 20i use, separate from product identity.

### Integration charging principle

A supported optional integration may carry a fixed Launch increment, a fixed
Ongoing increment for recurring support/reporting, or no extra charge when burden
is negligible. Charging should follow repeatable service burden, not open-ended
custom estimates. No price, fixed entitlement or final commercial term is set here.

Normal variation/minor complexity should be absorbed, not automatically charged.
The increment principle applies when material supported burden actually justifies
it and is agreed, rather than creating plan-tier creep.

## Progressive onboarding and selective customer effort

The system can work with limited information, but accurate business knowledge
materially improves the attainable outcome. Missing information stays unknown;
limited input never authorizes plausible invented facts or unsupported claims.
Avoid exhaustive labor-intensive discovery as the normal service model.

| Level | Intended operation |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial V1 structured interview | Reuse the versioned/resumable intake and immutable submissions, activation/customer foundation and confirmation model. Collect material identity, offerings/audiences, goals, Assets, operational and site knowledge for near-finished generation; separate confirmed facts, claims, preferences, recommendations and gaps. Target follow-up without repeated questions. The full interview remains incomplete and is required for V1. |
| Progressive enrichment | Ask the highest-value missing questions when relevant to content, business changes or a recommendation. Reuse approved knowledge rather than repeat intake. |
| Operator-assisted exception | Additional help when a customer genuinely needs it, with scope/time visible; not the default assumption of a large discovery engagement. |

Local/synthetic proving can use operator-assisted intake before portal completion.
Commercial V1 still requires the minimal customer portal. That implementation
bridge does not define the target workload or justify routine
manual reconstruction. The [onboarding model](onboarding-optimization.md) owns
trust, conditional questions and authorized canonical writes.

**The system should do work continuously; the customer should contribute
selectively.** Ask for facts or judgment the system cannot reasonably know: “Has
this service changed?”, “Which of these three topics matters most?”, “Answer these
three questions so we can draft,” or “Did these leads become customers?” Do not
require customers to maintain another complex dashboard or marketing workflow.

## Operating cadences

The [Conversion Architecture contract](conversion-architecture.md#post-conversion-confidence-and-response-expectations)
requires truthful post-action confirmation, next steps and response expectations.
An urgent visitor intent does not create availability or a callback commitment.
Confirm response responsibility/capacity before publishing timing; internal review
or service-minute hypotheses are not prospect-facing response promises. Existing
customers may need a distinct supported service path without competing with acquisition.

The [opportunity and optimization model](opportunity-and-optimization-model.md)
owns evidence-driven action selection and maintain/watch/intervene assessment.
Calendar reviews do not require content quotas or changes to healthy Pages. Base
Ongoing supports sustainable growth within customer capacity, not maximum activity.

Cadences describe intended service behavior as capabilities become available;
they are not implemented schedulers, publication quotas or contractual response times.

| Cadence | Intended value cycle |
| --- | --- |
| Continuous | Observe operation/uptime and primary first-party site/Page/Action activity, accepted/delivered Contacts and available attribution/outcome feedback. Monitor content, stale facts and opportunities; optional later GA4/Search Console may enrich evidence. No routine customer action required. |
| Monthly | Concise account of what happened, leads/Contact activity, known/confirmed outcomes, improvements and attention needs; 1–3 prioritized recommendations, draft/review items and targeted questions where needed. No analytics dump or artificial quota when evidence is insufficient. |
| Quarterly | Broader view of service/topic performance, accumulated content effects, useful channels, important underperforming Pages, business/positioning priorities, external-presence accuracy and next-period recommendations. A meeting is optional when it adds value. |
| Triggered | Respond to meaningful traffic/lead-volume changes, emerging search visibility, high activity with weak conversion, recurring questions, new business/service facts, seasonal opportunities, stale critical information or sufficiently supported content opportunities. Do not wait for a calendar cycle when attention is useful sooner. |

Calibrate triggers to available evidence and customer context; sparse data should
not cause confident anomaly claims or noisy prompts. Triggered action may mean
review, a customer question or an operational response, not automatic publication
or changes to protected business facts. Missing Google data should be disclosed
without interrupting independent first-party reporting. Technical-health and
business-conversion signals remain separate under the measurement contract.

## Minimal Commercial V1 customer experience

The incomplete minimal portal must cover onboarding/interview, site/content review,
corrections/feedback, approvals, current service/site status, concise reports and
change/support requests. Use supported HostShop links/handoff for account/billing
where practical, with capability/authority proof before promises. Do not build
native billing, CRM, sophisticated account administration, unrestricted page
building or broad infrastructure self-service for V1.

Ordinary customers can use the service without WordPress Admin. Keep the customer
experience simple for reporting new or corrected facts, answering targeted
questions, sharing changes to priorities, goals and audiences, requesting content,
presentation or operational changes, raising account concerns, reporting lead
quality or business outcomes, and reviewing recommendations and approvals.
Evaluate simple portal interactions, guided requests, structured updates,
asynchronous communication and Operator mediation. The exact channel and workflow
mix remains open. Capture source and context once, route to the appropriate owner
and use the [existing fact and change review](customer-journey-and-change-management.md#business-change-is-expected).
Requests and feedback carry no implied authority to change facts or publish.

Commercial V1 also needs operational communications sufficient for onboarding,
approvals and support; one reliable visitor conversion path, durable Contact/lead
acceptance/delivery state and verified customer/operator handoff, with appropriate
abuse/privacy/retention controls. CRM and Mailchimp integration are not required.

## Value and conversion reporting

Use plain language: what happened, what generated useful activity, what became a
lead, what became a customer where known, what improved and what should happen
next. Link recommendations to evidence and purpose; show unknowns and the observation
period. Customers should not have to interpret raw analytics. Do not invent ROI,
equate publication volume with value or claim every improvement caused a sale.

The existing [conversion ladder](measurement-and-analytics.md#conversion-ladder-and-business-value-reports)
remains authoritative: Observed means a directly measured visitor/Action/form/Contact
event; Attributed is a reasonable source/campaign/channel association; Customer
confirmed is reported business value; Unknown means the downstream result is not
reported. These levels do not establish full-funnel attribution.

### First-party lead signals and outcome confirmation

Lead/Contact conversion is a first-class planned concept for the initial ICP.
Direct signals include accepted Contact submissions, click-to-call, click-to-email,
and explicitly designated quote/request, booking/request or other lead Actions.
A request Action click does not prove a completed booking or qualified lead. Track
those distinctions rather than display every click as a new inquiry. Existing
Action destinations are not changed or invented for measurement purposes.

Make downstream confirmation easy without requiring a CRM. When actual evidence
supports it, a prompt might say: “Five leads came through MioPages this month.
What happened?” For each known lead, offer **Became customer**, **Still open**,
**Did not convert**, or **Not sure**. Do not attach these choices to anonymous
phone/email clicks as if identifiable leads had been captured. Nonresponse remains
Unknown; customer confirmation should be minimal, attributable and correctable
under appropriate access/privacy/retention rules.

The [opportunity model](opportunity-and-optimization-model.md#explainable-prioritization-and-sustainable-growth)
adds a complementary good-prospect / poor-fit / unclear quality dimension. Lead
quality and sales outcome are distinct: a good prospect may not buy. Both feedback
paths remain planned, minimal and subject to the same customer authority/privacy rules.

This is a planned lightweight workflow, not an implemented CRM or outcome table.
Future CRM integration may improve it automatically without becoming a prerequisite.
The [growth loop](content-growth-loop.md) owns grounded content work and review;
the [measurement architecture](measurement-and-analytics.md) owns event meaning,
normalization, attribution and provider failure.

## Responsibility layers

**Automate observation and preparation. Human-review judgment. Customer-approve
business truth.** The layers compose; system preparation is not publication approval.

| Layer | Intended work |
| --- | --- |
| System-automatic | Authorized measurement collection, source classification, lead-event tracking, content monitoring, opportunity scoring, report assembly, trusted-knowledge drafts, stale-information reminders and anomaly detection. Preserve uncertainty; these automations are planned, not current implementations. |
| Operator-reviewed | Prioritize recommendations, review ambiguity and draft quality, resolve conflicting facts with the customer, handle supported infrastructure/content-transfer exceptions, and assess material trust/positioning decisions. Operator review cannot manufacture factual authority. |
| Customer-approved | Final business claims, material service/pricing/availability changes, externally published AI-assisted content, major positioning/brand changes and downstream outcomes only the customer knows. Approval remains scoped to an actual reviewed outcome. |

The [growth approval contract](content-growth-loop.md#human-review-and-publication-approval)
permits an authorized customer/operator reviewer for initial publication; customer
business authority governs factual/material decisions, including authorized delegated
approval where explicitly established. Do not infer delegation from an account
assignment or an admin role. Broad automation policy cannot override protected facts,
pricing approval or initial human publication review. AI has no independent responsibility.

## Operator and team model

The initial service may have one operator. Single-operator operation is the simplest
case of multi-operator operation, not an architecture in which one identity always
does everything. A lightweight future model should support operator identity, a
finite role, customer/account assignment, allowed action categories, attention-item
ownership and bounded audit/provenance. No workforce-management system is proposed.

| Provisional role | Scope concept |
| --- | --- |
| Owner / Admin | Full service administration within enforced tenant/security and customer-approval boundaries; not permission to bypass business authority. |
| Content Reviewer | Recommendations, drafts and publishing-side review within assigned scope. |
| Technical Operator | Hosting, deployment, supported integrations and operational issues. |
| Account / Success | Customer prompts, follow-up and outcome-reporting coordination. |
| Analyst / Viewer | Authorized measurement/report visibility; read-only by default. |

These are provisional role concepts, not implemented permissions or required launch
UI. One operator can simulate several roles during testing; actual caller identity
and authorized scope must still be clear. Production authentication, least privilege
and tenant isolation remain P7/P19 obligations even before a team exists. See
[management security](security-operations.md#management-access-and-tenant-isolation).

As customer count grows, specialization and role-based attention routing can increase
without changing the customer model. Preserve a consistent customer experience;
do not require customers to navigate the internal team structure.

### Human-centered work orchestration and active-role context

**Approved product direction, November 7, 2026; not an implemented feature or authorization to deploy.** The owner reviewed the initial local C0 Operator overview favorably. Its navigation is rudimentary, and at 50 accounts the Operator should not have to guess where to start. Make the Operator landing experience an **actionable work queue**, not a customer directory or analytics dashboard: what requires attention, why, relevant evidence, next action and appropriate resource. Support immediate exceptions, grouped upcoming/routine activity and a searchable account directory. Healthy or quiet should never imply verified health when measurement and monitoring evidence are absent. Validate information density with clearly labeled representative 50-account cases and ultimately a 100–150-presence service.

**Respect professional attention and prevent overload.** Initially the owner may be the only Operator; additional people may join soon. Minimize mundane checks, duplicate entry, interruptive notifications, manual task grooming, administrative timesheets and busywork. The owner has found Salesforce, Asana and similar tools excessively burdensome to administer. Judge success by useful completed outcomes, customer value and sustainable effort, not tasks or status changes recorded. Capture lightweight effort, capacity and service economics to support resource planning without employee surveillance.

**Best-resource execution, one shared workflow.** Coordinate deterministic system automation (scheduled checks, validation, safe predictable operations), bounded AI/API help (interpretation, prioritization, grouping, drafting, next-action/resource recommendations) and human judgment (creative fit, customer relationship, exceptions, sensitive decisions). Choose a suitable authorized resource using business impact, urgency, confidence, effort, cost, availability and competence. A work item need not become an Operator task just because an observation exists. Group related signals, distinguish priority from workflow status and approval authority, and let approved routines complete without an unnecessary human touch. Keep authoritative customer requests, opportunities, proposals/revisions, media reviews, releases, approvals and incidents in their existing systems; the coordination view references those records instead of becoming another independent task database.

**Early bounded AI triage and cost accounting.** Bring API-assisted prioritization into an early practical Operator iteration, once real scoped attention/proposal evidence is connected. At first it *advises* on rationale, confidence, urgency, business impact, effort, next action and best executor; it does not autonomously approve customer facts, incur unbounded spend or publish. Prefer deterministic rules when adequate. Associate paid calls with account/work purpose, protected context and usage evidence; set conservative budgets, retry and failure controls, and track attributable AI cost, actual useful outcome and approximate human service effort. Compare service effort and costs with account economics. Expansion to automatic dispatch or protected execution needs separate policy and authority decisions; no general autonomous-agent framework is required.

**Lightweight guidance, escalation and reassignment.** From the existing work, an Operator should be able to ask a question or send a decision 'up the ladder' to the appropriate authorized person, specialist or AI resource without assembling an Asana-like project. Pass scoped evidence, previous attempts, open question, applicable boundaries and required decision authority with the handoff, and return the answer to the original workflow. Recommend or perform reassignment only within explicitly approved limits when another resource can resolve it better or sooner. Avoid duplicated tasks, reconstructing context and frequent manual status updates. A referral or model suggestion never substitutes for a required customer or release approval.

**One login, multiple authorized capacities.** Separate authenticated human *identity*, their assigned *roles*, and their current *active role*. The initial owner can hold multiple roles and switch capacities within one browser session; always make signed-in identity and active role visible. An active-role selector may present only server-authorized choices and must change the effective server-enforced permissions/scope, not merely a label. Keep tenant/customer boundaries, least privilege, approval separation and attribution of who acted in which capacity. Acting as both Operator and Owner does not silently grant customer authority, allow unapproved publication or permit self-approval where independent approval is required. Existing provisional Owner/Admin, Content Reviewer, Technical Operator, Account/Success and Analyst/Viewer concepts remain candidates, not a finalized permission matrix. The current loopback-only, read-only C0 interface has **no authentication**, so it cannot claim real role security; a future labeled demonstration must be explicitly simulated until P7 identity and authorization are connected.

**Integrated, browser-first work.** MioPages owns the coherent Operator and Customer browser experiences. WordPress is a supported specialist composition/delivery environment, not a mandatory customer admin interface; avoid unnecessary third-party CRM, project or time-management services. Evaluate specialist providers only for clear benefit and bounded integration. When the Operator needs a visual on a customer page, selection (Pixabay, customer-supplied or generated), source/rights checks, fit/crop, exact page preview, approval and eventual delivery should be one contextual workflow, not disconnected scripts or services. Pixabay discovery has been proven live, but a usable stock picker, authorized download and production delivery are not implemented. Preserve the accepted C0 brand design rather than forcing stock where it does not improve the page; revisit image selection promptly on suitable customer-style pages.

**Near-term implementation sequence and open policy.** Review the first C0 Operator view with the owner, then connect tenant-scoped real content proposal revisions and exact preview evidence in account detail. Improve evidence-led attention grouping and navigation, test scale using clearly labeled fixtures, then pilot bounded AI triage, contextual guidance/handoff and role-aware experiences. Integrate real page-context media work into this same surface. Determine future role-permission matrix, reassignment authority, workload/capacity cues, escalation/notification timing and AI budget thresholds from operating evidence. Nothing above authorizes hosted writes, external AI calls, new credentials or irreversible production actions.

The [local console](development-control.md#persistent-identity-and-simulated-role-switching)
demonstrates one identity switching between two example roles in a shared
interface. Role changes affect emphasis and shortcuts. Recorded C0 evidence
and access remain independent of the browser preference. C0 proposal status
continues to be unknown pending an authenticated tenant connection.

### Founder-led customer stewardship and contextual decisions

**Owner-approved direction, November 8, 2026; interactive implementation pending.** MioPages' customer communication and account-management model deliberately draws on the founder's more than 30 years of client relationship experience, including the judgment reflected in product-development discussions. This is a distinctive service standard, not a generic CRM template or a requirement that every future Operator supply equivalent deep strategic input. The founder guides and approves material refinements to relationship principles, examples, message tone, judgment heuristics and escalation boundaries. Other Operators should be able to execute confidently within those standards, use contextual guidance, flag exceptions and ask for a decision without maintaining elaborate administrative processes. Avoid treating informal conversational judgments as automatically approved policy; curate and validate examples before promoting them.

**Decisions belong with the work.** At an account, interview question, proposed content change or media item, show a concise AI/system recommendation, grounded reason, meaningful uncertainty, likely impact and suggested next action alongside the underlying evidence. Make details, metadata, thumbnails, discussion and alternative actions expandable in place. Where a natural group contains ten decisions, the system may *preselect* low-risk, straightforward recommendations for the Operator to check; the Operator can confirm nine with one **Approve selected (9)** action and expand the tenth for closer attention. Each selected item must retain its own decision evidence, scope, acting identity/role and audit record. Do not combine different approval authorities into one bulk action, imply that preselection is consent, or confuse accepting a review recommendation with customer factual approval or publication authority.

**Turn brief feedback into contextual customer care.** For example, an Operator reviewing a customer-supplied repair photo may say, "The image is blurry. Ask for a replacement." The system should unselect or defer that image, draft an appropriate customer message directly beneath the item, and let the Operator quickly inspect, edit, approve or reject it. Prefer a natural, helpful message over a rigid form or another task-management workflow. Before external communication, check identity, facts, claims, tone, context and accidental or AI-typical awkwardness; these checks reduce risk but do not guarantee correctness. The owner expects an Operator to eyeball all material sent to a client for approval or published in the early phase, with explicit human confirmation before customer-facing sends. Any later low-risk automatic sending or publishing needs a separate authorization policy.

**Protect customer confidence and service consistency.** Treat embarrassing or implausible AI errors as a trust problem, even if technically low-risk. Account-specific knowledge and customer voice must remain scoped; do not turn individual customer messages into unapproved cross-customer learning. Use founder-reviewed service patterns and explicitly approved corrections to improve shared operating guidance without relying on every Operator to train or supervise AI. Measure the effort, error rate, outcome and customer impact of these interactions to refine the approach without creating a bureaucracy.

### Exception-driven attention queue

**Approved direction, November 7, 2026.** Internal customer account review extends
the existing cadences, opportunity management and attention queue. It considers
customer requests, supported account and operational signals, business context and
emerging opportunities, including significant developments the customer has not
raised. The aim is to reduce day-to-day Operator work for healthy accounts and
reserve human effort for decisions, relationships and meaningful exceptions.

Review importance to the customer's objectives, business performance, trust or
service continuity, likely effort to investigate or act, urgency, whether the
activity is ordinary or exceptional, confidence in the evidence, and who can act.
Use those factors to choose an appropriate response.

| Condition | Intended handling |
| --- | --- |
| Routine and predictable | Use an established, appropriately authorized routine where practical. Ordinary activity can complete without a manual task. |
| Useful and nonurgent | Record and consolidate related observations. Revisit in the appropriate operating cadence or when evidence changes. |
| Important, unusual or consequential | Prompt the responsible Operator or customer with evidence, business context, likely impact and a proposed action or decision. |

Retain conceptual routing states of **no action**, **customer prompt**, **operator
review** and **urgent operator attention**. Keep severity and routing separate from
workflow status. Group related observations around the underlying issue. An
actionable item should identify the account, evidence, why it matters, what
decision or action is needed, likely effort and owner, with timing and bounded
provenance as applicable. Keep prompts concise and evidence scoped to the
recipient's authorized account and role. Reuse established workflows and avoid
duplicate entry, noisy alerts and a general-purpose administration dashboard.

Use the [opportunity model](opportunity-and-optimization-model.md#opportunity-lifecycle-and-customer-view)
for optimization candidates and the [business-change process](customer-journey-and-change-management.md#business-change-is-expected)
when evidence suggests the account needs redirection. Consequential publishing,
authoritative knowledge changes and other protected actions require their existing
approvals regardless of urgency or recommendation.

The [local Operator overview](development-control.md#local-operator-overview-and-c0-account-review)
applies this attention-first layout to the reviewed C0 snapshot. It groups a
recorded evidence decision above the account directory and links to a page
preview. Live attention routing and queue persistence remain planned.

Detailed design remains open, including review triggers and timing, which routines
may execute under policy, classification, prompt cadence, assignment and escalation,
integration with opportunity management and V1 phasing. The existing small V1
attention workflow remains the planned baseline. The broader review direction has
no implementation, new response commitment or authority to automate protected actions.

## Service economics and planning hypotheses

The November 5 discussion explored roughly $200–$250/month for Ongoing, with $225
used illustratively. The owner raised small-business acquisition resistance even
at a defensible price. Keep this as a willingness-to-pay hypothesis alongside the
existing working commercial model. Validate perceived value, acquisition friction,
Launch costs, continuing obligations, sustainable service effort and margins before
setting terms. The illustrative Ongoing figure is separate from the working Launch
installment. Published pricing and customer entitlements require their own decision.
See the [working commercial model](progressive-onboarding-and-launch-readiness.md#miopages-working-commercial-model).

Use the [effort-budgeting model](opportunity-and-optimization-model.md#effort-budgeting-and-campaign-escalation)
to distinguish value, next priority and the bounded internal service envelope.
Include system/operator work, customer review, implementation and future maintenance.
The time hypotheses below are neither numeric entitlement caps nor “hours included.”
Surface large opportunities for scope review as supported expansion, a bounded
Campaign, operator review or outside scope; do not silently absorb unlimited labor
or automatically surcharge routine included evolution. Calibrate with C0/early
customer evidence and preserve agreed commercial/approval boundaries.

Track Launch operator hours, **service minutes per customer per month**, infrastructure
cost, AI/API usage, exceptional support time, incidents and content/review activity.
Future acquisition cost can supplement these. Service minutes are the strongest
operating workload metric: human time should concentrate where judgment adds value,
not where software can reliably do the work. Capture exceptional time as well as
routine time rather than hide it from averages.

Evaluate efficiency with routine Operator minutes per active account, effort and
elapsed time per meaningful request, avoidable interventions, attention-item volume
and usefulness, missed consequential exceptions, effectiveness of approved routines,
customer effort to maintain accurate knowledge, and observed outcomes and customer
satisfaction. These are candidate measures. A larger account base should be able to
benefit from centralized capabilities and reusable workflows without requiring a
proportional increase in routine human work. Set any future targets from evidence.

| Non-canonical planning target | Current hypothesis to test |
| --- | --- |
| Normal / Standard Launch | Approximately 3–6 operator hours. |
| Expanded Launch | Approximately 6–10 operator hours. |
| Normal Ongoing | Ideally under 45 operator minutes/customer/month. |
| More complex Ongoing | Ideally under 60 operator minutes/customer/month. |

These ranges are internal planning assumptions, not contractual promises, platform
invariants, staffing commitments or permission to skip necessary review. Measure
actual workload and outcomes. Persistent overruns may indicate wrong service
boundaries, insufficient automation, unsuitable pricing or poor customer fit;
investigate those causes instead of relying on unpaid bespoke effort. D6 already
owns the durable economics principle; no actual prices are established here.

### Cost-to-serve and provider expenses

Track real cost-to-serve as the service matures, alongside operator effort. Separate
**fixed platform costs** from **variable/per-customer costs**, and record whether
each cost is **absorbed** by MioPages or **passed through** transparently. Cost driver
and commercial treatment are different dimensions; avoid counting one expense twice.

Include actual 20i Managed Cloud/runtime, Supabase/PostgreSQL, AWS backup/storage,
email, domains, paid SSL exceptions, applicable CDN/provider fees and other external
services. Record provider/item, observation period, actual amount/billing basis and
customer attribution where applicable. These are management records, not a new
accounting system, assumed vendor prices or public service entitlements.

For [paid SSL exceptions](platform-service-architecture.md#dns-and-tls--https), track
the number of exception customers, affected customer, unit/annual certificate cost
and absorbed versus passed-through treatment. Initial absorption of a modest
annual cost is allowed by the operating direction; later transparent pass-through
needs appropriate commercial agreement. Neither policy changes canonical pricing.

Use actual expenses and service effort in recurring margin, pricing and plan-design
reviews. Repeated exceptions may justify automation or architectural change, but
review total implementation/maintenance burden and risk rather than certificate
price alone. At 10/100/1000 customers ask whether SSL exceptions justify automation,
operator-assisted Google grants are a bottleneck, accounting/entity/insurance
processes need to mature, and provider costs remain sensible. Set no customer-count or revenue threshold
without evidence. Material pressure can promote an existing deferred item to P0
through the [register's review workflow](deferred-architecture.md#slice-workflow),
not by silently changing prices or approving infrastructure work.

## Commercial structure, retention and fit

One broadly inclusive Ongoing service absorbs normal updates, ordinary additions/
removals, useful content/core-site/conversion improvements, reporting, optimization
and operations within the effort envelope. More posts are a prioritization question,
not a quota plan: do useful work within normal Ongoing or recommend a bounded Campaign
when a real accelerated initiative materially exceeds it. See the
[current working commercial/exit direction](progressive-onboarding-and-launch-readiness.md#miopages-working-commercial-model)
for MioPages assumptions, Ongoing from go-live and nonpayment/ownership safeguards;
final agreement/legal/accounting review is still required.

Current non-final direction is a fixed/bounded Launch component and a recurring
Ongoing component. A likely pattern is Standard Launch, Expanded Launch, one primary
Ongoing service and bounded optional integrations/value-adds. Avoid a maze of
packages; scope and recurring burden should remain comprehensible. Pricing, terms,
service levels and final packaging need separate commercial validation.

Ongoing's value is that the system keeps helping the presence improve, not primarily
payment to prevent a website from breaking. Reliable operation matters, alongside
recommendations, content, optimization, business updates, conversion refinement and
demonstrated progress. Keep an understandable exit path; lock-in is not retention.

Good fit: small organizations with real expertise/business knowledge, limited desire
or capacity for web management, willingness to use bounded design/integration choices
and interest in measurable improvement. Poor fit: arbitrary HTML/CSS, exhaustive pixel
control, continuous bespoke redesign, custom applications, customer-only integrations
without a reusable support path, or full CRM/marketing-automation replacement at launch.
The [MioPages ICP](miopages-c0-product-model.md#initial-customer-hypothesis) is a proving
hypothesis, not an industry or head-count invariant.

## MioPages C0 proving work

Use MioPages to test Standard Launch scope/time assumptions, progressive onboarding,
attention routing, value reports, the conversion ladder, easy outcome confirmation,
operator-role simulation, service-minute tracking, bounded integration decisions
and direct Launch-to-Ongoing continuity. Record gaps and revise hypotheses based on
observed burden/value, not a handcrafted showcase exempt from the platform rules.
MioPages-specific account/provider/content choices do not become platform defaults.
