# MioPages business understanding and Presence Direction

**Customer review draft · November 15, 2026**

MioPages is preparing to launch a managed Web Presence service for small businesses. The strongest reason to choose it is that the work has an owner after launch. The business owner supplies facts and makes consequential decisions. MioPages helps decide what the website needs to accomplish, builds within an agreed scope, and keeps improving the presence as useful evidence becomes available.

Our recommendation is to explain that relationship first and give visitors two concrete ways into it. A business with a website can start by asking what to retain, improve or replace. A startup can start by deciding what its first customers need to understand and how they should make contact. Both paths lead to the same service. [Read the complete proposed website](c0-researched-website-candidate.md)

## Our understanding of your business

**Established direction.** MioPages is retiring its legacy website-builder, self-managed QR, standalone hosting/email and broad support offers. It is creating one managed service with two parts. Launch establishes a credible presence around the real business, including useful content, a working visitor path, and an initial measurement baseline where supported. Ongoing keeps the presence accurate and useful through operation, prioritized updates, content and conversion improvements, and understandable reporting. The existing HostShop portal continues as a separate utility for legacy account holders. [Business brief](miopages-c0-business-brief.md) · [Retirement decision](https://github.com/R-Levin/myomaton-web/issues/10#issuecomment-6740683772)

**Who it can help.** The founder confirmed that startups belong alongside established service, trade, professional and technical businesses. Established businesses remain a sensible group to learn from first. An existing-site handoff could be a strong acquisition path, but it remains an option to validate. A new business with a clear offer may need a small first site and a reliable first inquiry path. An established firm may need to explain better what it already does, protect a useful destination or replace a site its staff no longer maintain. The first group may ask whether it needs a continuing service yet. The second may worry about losing a domain, breaking a referral link or replacing the tools people already use. These are proposed buying scenarios to validate, not observed customer behavior. [Audience correction](miopages-c0-product-model.md#initial-customer-hypothesis) · [Strategy recovery](https://github.com/R-Levin/myomaton-web/blob/2c6a41f/docs/c0-strategy-recovery-and-intelligent-onboarding-2026-11-10.md)

**What should be easy to see.** An owner should know what they will supply, what MioPages will take on and which decisions require checking together. Ordinary work belongs in Standard Launch or Ongoing. Unusually complex but supported migration or integration can require an agreed Expanded scope. A bespoke app or unsupported commerce is outside the ordinary offer. Exact commercial terms, capacity, and when an export can be supplied need confirmation before they become promises. The older preliminary prices and later working figures belong in the commercial record. [Working commercial model](progressive-onboarding-and-launch-readiness.md#miopages-working-commercial-model)

## What the research suggests a buyer needs to decide

A close comparable, SiteCare360, packages a build with continuing management and explains ownership and exit terms in its FAQ. SiteUpdater leads with an existing-site refresh and asks for a site address before a preview. SiteCare, a technical WordPress care provider, explains which work fits an ordinary edit and where a separate project begins. A UK comparable, Site Commander Studio, makes its project selection, steps, pricing and later updates easy to find. Their claims describe those providers’ offers. They supply no proof of sales, search demand, satisfaction or results. [SiteCare360](https://sitecare360.com/) · [Ownership FAQ](https://sitecare360.com/faq) · [SiteUpdater](https://siteupdater.com/) · [SiteCare](https://sitecare.com/faqs/) · [Site Commander Studio](https://www.sitecommanderstudio.com/websites)

These observations changed the proposed design in three ways.

1. **Give continuity its own page.** A handoff prospect needs to know what happens to pages, links, forms and account access before committing. An existing URL is useful for that decision. A new-business prospect can explain their offer without supplying one.
2. **Show Ongoing as work a buyer can recognize.** Explain what happens when services change, referrals arrive at the wrong page or a form fails. A brief example can explain how to observe, decide, revise and check again. It cannot serve as a case study.
3. **Show ownership and fit in the decision flow.** Tell prospects which facts and approvals they control, where they can expect ordinary supported help, and when both sides need to agree a different scope. Discuss retained assets and exit terms before commitment.

The project’s development proofs show pieces of the method. They do not establish outcomes for new-service customers. Use a clearly labeled illustrative Review to demonstrate the work. Add historical examples only after verifying contribution, dates and permission for the exact proposed use. Keep existing holdbacks. A separately documented result can say what was measured and under which conditions once there is real evidence. [Proof guidance](miopages-c0-business-brief.md#proof-and-trust)

## Presence Direction

**Positioning.** Lead with a useful business presence whose operation and improvement have a responsible owner. Avoid shorthand about hosting, AI or WordPress in the main offer. Explain those choices where they help a buyer judge fit or continuity.

**Visitor paths.** Give an undecided prospect a Free Review that is useful on its own. Give a ready prospect a direct scope path, with a conversation option for a specific question. Keep the separate HostShop sign-in in utility navigation and out of prospect conversion counts. The full business understanding report follows deeper onboarding; it is a different deliverable from the Free Review. Both entry paths should retain useful information already supplied. [Conversion architecture](conversion-architecture.md) · [Onboarding knowledge transfer](paid-onboarding-knowledge-transfer-contract.md)

**Current site.** Replace the legacy offer explanation and sales navigation. Preserve the brand and the HostShop link where appropriate. Review individual gallery items and useful evergreen material rather than copying the old presentation. Existing contact, policies, legacy URLs and QR dependencies each need their own continuity decision before cutover. The old site’s labels do not establish the new offer or authorize changes. [Current homepage](https://miopages.com/) · [Public site inventory](c0-adaptive-onboarding-walkthrough.md#the-existing-site-review) · [HostShop sign-in](https://my.miopages.com/login)

**Visual direction.** Use the accepted service-led v3 as a design reference. Keep its quiet hierarchy, Source Sans 3, conceptual Hero and illustrative report direction subject to the exact asset review. Use annotated diagrams to explain handoff and Ongoing when they convey more than a photo. No selected stock photograph is part of this Home. Use clear section headings, readable contrast and visible labels, focus states and error feedback. [Accepted v3 snapshot](wordpress-c0-v3-mapping.md) · [Accessible images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/decision-tree/) · [Accessible forms](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/)

**Search and content.** Begin with the questions a prospect brings: “Who will manage our website?”, “Can we keep the things that already work?”, “What should my first website do?”, and “What support continues after launch?” These are qualitative intent hypotheses. Explain the answers on distinct, linked pages, then use genuine inquiries and lawful aggregate search evidence to decide which supporting guides deserve separate publication. Use descriptive titles, internal links and visible information that matches any structured data. Search or AI inclusion is never guaranteed. [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) · [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

**Ongoing priorities.** Establish a repeatable baseline for entry pages, action selection, successfully accepted inquiries and delivery to the responsible recipient. Ask customers which inquiries were a fit when the workflow supports that feedback. A click, accepted inquiry and confirmed outcome answer different questions. A useful first experiment could clarify one call to action or add one missing qualification detail, then compare the same kind of traffic or inquiries over suitable periods. No performance trend is established at this stage. [Measurement contract](measurement-and-analytics.md) · [Growth loop](content-growth-loop.md)
