# Opportunity management and sustainable optimization

## Purpose, ownership and status

**Recommend the best next Web Presence action, not merely the next piece of content.**
Content marketing is a core Ongoing capability, but is one response to an opportunity,
not the purpose of every recommendation. Base Ongoing supports sustainable growth,
not maximum activity or demand the customer cannot responsibly absorb.

This document owns opportunity classification, prioritization, effort budgeting,
post-action evaluation and learning boundaries. It is a durable product/operating
model, not an implemented queue, scheduler, scoring formula, schema, service-level
promise or autonomous marketing engine.

| Status | Actual boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Implemented foundations | Canonical business/content records, bounded Page/Section rendering, Navigation, Actions, managed media and development-gated Contact persistence/hooks supply inputs and eventual action surfaces. They do not implement analytics, opportunity persistence, ranking, effort policy or evaluation. Local media-acquisition/operator-QC tooling is not this optimization workflow. |
| Planned core / pre-launch | Customer-specific opportunity queue, explainable prioritization, effort budgeting, maintain/watch/intervene assessment, measurement contracts, scheduled post-action evaluation and customer-specific learning. Apply existing implementation gates before the corresponding capability is activated or offered. |
| Future | Campaign automation beyond basic supported composition, aggregate cross-customer learning, category benchmarking, automated learning-driven factor weighting and advanced predictive scoring. These are not initial launch requirements. |
| Separate review required | Campaign scope/economics and any cross-customer learning privacy/legal boundary. No commercial terms or production data-sharing permission is created here. |

The [content-growth loop](content-growth-loop.md) owns grounded drafting, actual-content
approval, publication and distribution. The [customer journey](customer-journey-and-change-management.md)
owns customer authority, business-change impact planning and commercial handoff.
The [service operations model](service-operations-model.md) owns scope, cadence and
operator-time hypotheses; the [measurement architecture](measurement-and-analytics.md)
owns signal meaning, collection, provenance and attribution limits.

The [Web Presence service model](web-presence-service-model.md) owns the proposition,
[onboarding/optimization](onboarding-optimization.md) owns trusted intake and
intelligence policy, and [MioPages C0](miopages-c0-product-model.md) supplies proving
context. The [capability catalog](platform-capabilities.md), [architecture register](deferred-architecture.md)
and [security/operations contract](security-operations.md) retain their status,
implementation and safety authority. No customer state is changed by this document.

## Core site and dynamic discovery layer

| Layer | Primary role |
| --- | --- |
| Core site | Relatively stable business truth, core offers/services/products, proof/trust, conversion paths and durable positioning. Stable does not mean immutable. |
| Dynamic content / growth layer | Discovery, search/topic exploration, supporting content, market/customer questions, emerging opportunities, qualified traffic growth and reinforcement of core offers. |

A blog is a bounded publishing container/dynamic discovery layer, not a goal in
itself. Classify work by purpose, not length or format. Not every opportunity becomes
an Article; nor should Articles substitute for missing core offer coverage or a
missing reusable site capability. Both layers draw on confirmed canonical knowledge.

### Opportunity types

| Type | Possible action, conditional on evidence and supported capability |
| --- | --- |
| Core-site | Correct or strengthen a durable Page or service explanation. |
| Content | Answer a relevant question with new or improved supporting content. |
| Conversion | Clarify the next step, qualification or supported Action path. |
| Content-to-core promotion | Recommend durable core coverage when discovery content reveals sustained relevant demand. |
| Internal connection / linking | Connect existing relevant content, offers and valid destinations more effectively. |
| Proof / trust | Gather, verify or better present permissioned evidence. |
| Audience / location | Improve coverage for a confirmed audience or supported location. |
| Offer / positioning | Propose clearer representation of an approved offer; confirm material business changes first. |
| Campaign | Evaluate a bounded focused initiative exceeding normal Ongoing scope. |
| Technical / discovery | Address a supported discovery, indexing or technical barrier without conflating technical health with business outcomes. |

Types describe the primary response and can be related; avoid duplicate queue items
for the same underlying issue. A recommendation is not proof that its required
publishing, media, integration or technical capability already exists.

## Evidence-driven cadence and existing-surface assessment

Useful work happens for a reason: evidence, business importance, timing, effort,
customer capacity and measurement feedback. Do not prescribe four posts per month,
weekly publication or another arbitrary output quota. Reporting/review cadences in
the operations model are checkpoints, not obligations to manufacture interventions.

Existing Pages/content may be assessed as:

| Assessment | Intended response |
| --- | --- |
| Maintain | Performing adequately; no evidence justifies change. Leave healthy material alone while normal operation continues. |
| Watch | Strategically important or currently healthy, but early decline, uncertainty or insufficient evidence warrants observation. Specify what would justify intervention. |
| Intervene | Evidence supports a meaningful problem/opportunity; create or re-rank an actionable recommendation. This is not authorization to apply it. |

Do not optimize for activity, or “fix” healthy content merely because it is old.
Static content does not inevitably decline. Bottom-of-funnel Pages can remain useful
for long periods; discovery content may warrant more frequent reassessment.

Possible signals include falling impressions/clicks or qualified traffic, worsening
lead quality, changed search intent, stale business truth, weaker conversion,
changed market/customer behavior or supporting content losing effectiveness.
Check coverage, source failure and observation windows before interpreting decline.
Known stale facts warrant correction through the business-change process even if
traffic is stable. Urgent security/availability defects still follow operational
incident handling; maintaining a healthy Page is not permission to neglect them.

Weak performance feeds this same recommendation system, not an alarming separate
customer-facing failure mode. Low qualified traffic, poor-fit inquiries, visibility
with weak CTR, traffic with weak conversion and weak offer coverage can raise
internal priority/severity or operator review. Explain the next useful action
constructively, preserving uncertainty and avoiding customer blame.

## Opportunity lifecycle and customer view

The practical internal lifecycle uses the following conceptual states:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Detected | A possible issue/opportunity has a recorded source or signal. |
| Qualified | Enough relevant evidence exists to consider it; missing prerequisites remain explicit. |
| Ranked | Evaluated against the stable decision framework and current context. |
| Now | Worth acting on next and fits the current service-effort envelope. |
| Later | Worthwhile, but lower priority, not timely or awaiting available capacity. |
| Watch | Interesting but insufficiently evidenced or not yet actionable; retain a review trigger. |
| Campaign candidate | Worthwhile but too large/focused for normal Ongoing; requires scope/economics review. |
| Approved | Required authorization for the specific proposed action/substance is obtained and current. |
| In progress | Authorized implementation is underway; preparation/drafting can also occur before publication approval under the content workflow. |
| Published / applied | The authorized action is actually live/effective; record actual timing and destination. |
| Measured | Post-action evaluation has been recorded against the measurement contract, including an inconclusive result when justified. |
| Closed | Completed, rejected, obsolete, superseded or no longer worthwhile; retain the reason under bounded history policy. |

This is not a mandatory linear sequence or a schema enum. Ranking can route to
Now/Later/Watch/Campaign candidate, and evidence, capacity or scope changes can
return an item for qualification/re-ranking. Not every detection deserves action.
Merge duplicates and close obsolete proposals rather than accumulate unlimited ideas.

Separate surface assessment, opportunity lifecycle, authorization, operator attention
and analytical result: a watched Page is not itself a queue item; “Now” does not mean
approved; “Working” is not a publication state. Draft/review iterations stay with
the existing content workflow rather than forcing a second editorial state machine.
An evaluation may be recorded as inconclusive while observation continues; it must
not imply that sufficient evidence of effect exists or force closure.

The customer sees a compact **Now / Later / Watching / Campaign** view with reasons
and simple next steps, not the full internal lifecycle. A Campaign candidate must
be distinguishable from an approved paid Campaign. Existing Approve / Request change /
Not now / Reject choices remain governed by the journey contract.

## Explainable prioritization and sustainable growth

Use a **stable decision framework with dynamic evidence-driven weighting**. Relevant
factors are:

- Expected effects on qualified traffic, lead volume and lead quality.
- Search/discovery opportunity and current performance weakness.
- Business importance of the affected offer and customer priority.
- Confidence, urgency/seasonality and prior related actions/results.
- System/implementation effort, operator effort and ongoing maintenance burden.
- Customer review burden and ability of the business to absorb growth.

No exact formula, numeric threshold, predictive certainty or “AI score: 87” is
defined here. Explain the evidence, expected value, uncertainty, effort and tradeoff.
For example, conditional on real evidence: “We recommend this now because search
visibility is increasing, the service matters to your business and effort is modest.”
Keep the rationale/context reviewable for customer trust, operator approval,
debugging and later learning.

Weights may change with current business context and evidence through explainable
rules/operator judgment. Automatically learning or tuning those weights from results
is a future capability, not required for the initial stable framework. Re-ranking
does not broaden approval scope or silently replace an approved action.

Customer capacity is a real constraint. If a service line is overloaded, deprioritize
its promotion, focus on another confirmed offer, recommend qualification/filtering,
seek a capacity-related business review or simply maintain/watch. Confirm capacity
context rather than infer staffing or availability as business truth. The objective
is sustainable business growth, not maximum lead volume.

Fair success signals include qualified traffic, actual form/contact leads and search
visibility growth, interpreted according to purpose. Conversion-rate changes,
useful content performance, referral/social contribution and customer-confirmed
outcomes may support them. Traffic volume alone is insufficient. Poor-fit leads
are a meaningful negative signal. Define what makes traffic relevant from available
evidence; an anonymous visit or click does not prove qualification.

Future customer feedback should distinguish **good prospect / poor fit / still
unclear or unknown** for identifiable leads. This quality dimension complements,
not replaces, the existing became-customer/still-open/did-not-convert/not-sure
outcome choices. A good prospect may not buy; an unreported outcome is not poor fit.
Do not attach identifiable-lead feedback to anonymous Action clicks.

## Effort budgeting and Campaign escalation

Separate what is worth doing, what should happen next and what fits the current
service envelope. Each opportunity pairs expected value with effort: system work,
operator work, customer review burden, implementation complexity and future
maintenance. Estimates retain uncertainty; measured effort improves later estimates.

Normal Ongoing executes the highest-value reasonable-effort actions that fit a
bounded internal envelope. Retain useful lower-priority work for later. Surface
valuable work outside the envelope explicitly rather than silently absorb or ignore
it. This document sets no numeric limits and does not sell “X hours included.”
Existing operator-minute figures are internal economics hypotheses, not entitlement
caps or substitutes for service policy. C0/early customers should calibrate value,
actual burden and the envelope before commitments are finalized.

Exceeding effort is a scope-review signal, not an automatic surcharge. Classify a
large action as supported expansion, Campaign candidate, operator review or outside
scope. Preserve routine evolution included in Ongoing and the journey's
[supported-expansion/bespoke boundary](customer-journey-and-change-management.md#included-evolution-supported-expansion-and-bespoke-work).
Explain material scope/economics and obtain agreement before extra work/charges.
Required safety/maintenance work cannot be dropped merely to favor promotional work;
route persistent effort overruns for operating-model review.

### Bounded Campaign, not a permanent high-growth tier

Exceptional opportunities may justify a bounded Campaign: a service launch,
seasonal opportunity, new location, event, promotion, focused offer, new market or
temporary strategic initiative. Do not create a “High Growth” tier merely because
one valuable opportunity is large.

A Campaign may use an existing supported surface or a synchronized secondary
presence/surface within the shared architecture, reuse canonical business knowledge
and the same measurement system, define its own scope/evaluation window and have
separate economics where justified. Roll results into customer reporting while
keeping source/surface and overlap distinguishable; do not double-count leads.

This does not implement provisioning, synchronization, Campaign management or new
host routing. Establish supported identity/URL ownership, factual propagation,
approval, measurement and eventual retirement/continuity before use. A Campaign
does not authorize bespoke work, a duplicate business-truth authority or unbounded
publication. Advanced Campaign automation remains future scope.

### Content-to-core promotion

Sustained relevant interest in an Article/topic may reveal that an existing customer
service is underrepresented. Recommend creating/expanding durable core coverage,
possibly promoting it into approved Navigation, strengthening the conversion path
and retaining useful supporting Articles. Do not automatically turn a popular topic
into a new offer, Page or Navigation entry. Confirm business relevance/capacity and
use existing plan, business-change and publication approvals. Learn exact promotion
criteria through C0/real evidence rather than set universal thresholds now.

## Measurement contract and post-action evaluation

Every meaningful approved action should have a measurement contract prepared before
application where practical, then completed with actual timing and results. This
is a bounded evaluative record, not a legal contract, an ROI promise or a new schema.

| Contract part | Required intent |
| --- | --- |
| Change and rationale | What changes, affected surface/entity, why, approved action/revision and expected outcome. |
| Primary metric(s) | Signals appropriate to the hypothesis, with source/definition and limits; avoid vanity volume alone. |
| Baseline | Relevant pre-action period/state, coverage and known confounders. If unavailable, record that explicitly rather than invent a baseline. |
| Evaluation window | Action-specific observation period starting from actual application/publication, with a review trigger. |
| Minimum evidence/confidence | What must be observed before a fair judgment; qualitative or bounded evidence criteria are acceptable without false statistical precision. |
| Result | Observations, data sufficiency, analytical state and uncertainty. |
| Next decision | Maintain, continue observing, refine, revert where appropriate, improve measurement, choose another opportunity or close with a reason. |

The conceptual loop is **baseline → action → observation window → evaluation →
learn → next decision**. Retain the rationale so it need not be rediscovered each
month. An urgent correction without a prior baseline must still record the gap;
do not delay necessary operational remediation merely to collect a control period.

Evaluation timing depends on action type: a technical correction may be verified
immediately/shortly, a conversion change may need days/weeks, search/content work
may take longer and a Campaign has a scope-specific window. These are examples,
not waiting-period promises. Technical fix verification is distinct from downstream
business effect. A window ending does not make sparse or confounded data sufficient.

| Result state | Meaning | Next response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Working | Evidence supports the intended effect, within stated attribution limits. | Maintain, keep measuring, avoid unnecessary tinkering and learn cautiously. |
| Promising | Positive early signal, insufficient evidence for a settled conclusion. | Continue observation; avoid premature changes. |
| Neutral | Sufficient time/data shows little meaningful change. | Reconsider the hypothesis and possibly select the next opportunity. |
| Underperforming | Evidence suggests the intended result is not occurring or something worsened. | Diagnose, refine/revert where appropriate and seek operator review when material. |
| Inconclusive | Insufficient, unavailable or confounded evidence prevents a fair judgment. | Wait, improve measurement or acknowledge the limit; manufacture no conclusion. |

These are analytical states, not customer blame labels or proven causal effects.
Refinement/reversion is a proposed action subject to existing authority and current
state; prior approval does not authorize arbitrary new claims or blind rollback.
Use “Too early to judge” or “Insufficient data” when appropriate. Small samples can
make percentage comparisons misleading; preserve counts, windows and limitations.
Do not invent statistical confidence or label missing data as neutral/zero.

### Context, confounders and discovery stages

Interpret actions alongside pricing changes, seasonality, capacity changes, major
business changes, Campaign launches, search-engine changes, outages/provider changes
and sales follow-up/lead-handling changes. Reuse the
[business-change reporting markers](measurement-and-analytics.md#business-change-context)
and journey history rather than create a competing event authority. Concurrent
interventions can make isolation impossible; record uncertainty instead of
attributing every improvement or decline to the most recent action.

For discovery content, success may progress through indexed/discovered → impressions
→ clicks/qualified visits → useful engagement → lead → good prospect/customer outcome
where known. This is an interpretive ladder, not a guaranteed tracked funnel.
Do not prematurely judge top-of-funnel work solely by direct leads. Visibility,
clicks, accepted inquiries, qualification and confirmed outcomes remain different
signals under the measurement contract; absent search data does not prove no discovery.

## Customer-specific and aggregate learning

Initial optimization works from customer-specific first-party data, approved
external intelligence, customer-confirmed outcomes, deterministic platform rules
and operator judgment. First-party collection/reporting is itself planned, not
made operational by this model. Optional external sources cannot become mandatory.

Results should improve that customer's recommendations: topics attracting relevant
interest, offers converting appropriately, paths producing poor-fit leads, useful
formats, repeatedly ineffective recommendation types, seasonality and capacity
constraints. Retain bounded rationale/results tied to customer-owned state and
source provenance, separate from confirmed business facts. One correlation is not
a universal rule, and learning does not grant new publication authority.

Future anonymous/aggregated patterns may inform recommendation defaults, CTA and
conversion patterns, onboarding heuristics, prioritization and ineffective action
types. **Learn from aggregate behavior; do not appropriate customer-specific
strategy, content or confidential success.** Never copy Customer A's content into
Customer B, expose private performance or relabel a customer's strategy as generic
platform knowledge.

Cross-customer learning is future-only and requires explicit privacy/legal and
security review before production: purpose, permitted data, authorization, retention,
aggregation/re-identification risk and any disclosures. Calling data anonymous is
not sufficient evidence of safety. Category benchmarking and learning-driven weight
tuning are also future, not initial dependencies. Initial optimization must work
without a cross-customer training/learning pool.

## Structured efficiency, bounded intelligence

**Use intelligence to increase leverage, not to remove structure.** The robust
model is a structured state machine with bounded intelligence at high-value points:

Signals → rules/thresholds → opportunity queue → selective AI interpretation →
approval → action → scheduled evaluation → learning → reprioritization.

Collect metrics, compare baselines, maintain opportunity state, apply validated
thresholds, schedule reviews, retain markers and rank known candidates deterministically
where practical. Selective AI can assist ambiguous interpretation, synthesis,
explanations, drafting, pattern recognition and proposed next actions. It cannot
invent evidence, approve truth, override effort/scope boundaries or directly publish
free-form output. Missing provider output must not erase queue/rationale state.

Operator attention concentrates on ambiguity, low confidence, material consequence,
scope/cost judgment, surprising results and customer approval needs. Routine logic
must remain inspectable and testable; do not build an opaque autonomous “marketing
brain.” Collection, access, provider data minimization and per-customer usage follow
the existing security/intelligence gates.

## Conceptual metadata, C0 proving and gates

An opportunity may eventually retain source/signal, affected business area and
Page/content/entity, type, expected value, effort, confidence, urgency, customer
priority, capacity/absorption context, rationale, lifecycle and approval states,
resulting action/artifact, baseline, evaluation window, measured result and next
decision. Preserve actual action time, evidence provenance and supersession where
needed to interpret those fields. These are conceptual requirements, not a full
schema; customer ownership, tenant isolation, export and bounded retention apply.

Use MioPages C0 to test detection/classification, maintain/watch/intervene,
Now/Later/Watch/Campaign routing, stable ranking factors, effort estimates and actual
operator work, measurement contracts/windows/results, sparse-data handling,
business-change context, customer-specific learning and explainability. Prove useful
decisions and sustainable burden, not idea count or output quotas. MioPages-specific
offers, data and time assumptions do not become platform invariants.

Reuse existing gates: P1/P2/P3 for guarded state/provenance, P4/P5/P7 for retention,
export and authority, P12 for measurement-contract/evaluation evidence, P13 for
explainable opportunities and bounded intelligence, P15 for effort-policy scope,
P26 for agreed extra commercial work, C2/C3/C7 for actual content publication and
D6 for observed service economics. Campaign serving also triggers applicable
P8/P9/P10/P14/P16 requirements; a scope label does not complete them. Future aggregate
learning must pass P7/P12/P13/P19 plus privacy/legal review before activation.
No new gate IDs, runtime capability, automatic approval or launch authorization is
created by this document.
