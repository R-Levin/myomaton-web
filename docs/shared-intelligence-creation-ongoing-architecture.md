# Shared AI intelligence and creation foundation — Launch and Ongoing

**Status:** Owner-directed architecture clarification, November 18, 2026. **Proposed implementation structure**, not an implemented module, live Ongoing engine, customer approval or provider transmission grant. Preserve existing working functions and correct them in place; no parallel demonstration system. This companion documents the **shared interfaces and deliberately different workflows** for initial Creation/Launch and continuous Ongoing. Existing specialist contracts keep their authority: [Business and service](web-presence-service-model.md), [knowledge](onboarding-optimization.md), [Conversion Architecture](conversion-architecture.md), [content-growth](content-growth-loop.md), [opportunity model](opportunity-and-optimization-model.md), [measurement](measurement-and-analytics.md), [approvals](customer-journey-and-change-management.md), [operations](service-operations-model.md), [architecture](platform-service-architecture.md), [security](security-operations.md), [implementation gates](deferred-architecture.md).

## Objective — as complete as practical now, iterated afterward

MioPages is one **AI-assisted managed Web Presence growth service**. Its intelligence should understand a real business and its market, create a persuasive original site, then **remain engaged with that same living customer knowledge** as evidence and circumstances change. Initial generation is not the end product; Ongoing is not a disconnected “AI writer” or scheduled content factory. Build a production-shaped **shared research → understanding → decision → creative proposal → review → WordPress delivery → measurement** foundation, with real source and authority handling, genuinely useful model judgment and new-structure generation. Improve model quality and heuristics through observed outputs, not by putting a superficial prompt or rigid template in place and calling the architecture complete.

**Crucial difference:** Launch is a broad **cold-start composition and baseline-establishment** exercise. Ongoing is a **delta-driven, hypothesis-led choice and intervention** over an existing, approved and measured Web Presence. Do not run the Launch page/site generator in a loop each month, automatically rewrite healthy content or assume that a new article is always the best change. Both modes reuse the same customer-owned knowledge, research/visual intelligence, brand governance, content/component compiler and protected review/delivery interfaces.

## Proposed module boundaries at existing seams

These are **logical interfaces**, not permission to introduce new microservices, duplicate PostgreSQL authorities or design a second demo. Prefer extending the corresponding existing implementation.

1. **Context and knowledge projection.** Read existing canonical Business Knowledge/Offerings, approved business brief and source history through the My Business/intake/canonical boundaries. Produce a purpose-scoped, versioned, **complete-enough** view of confirmed facts, staffed offer, audience, visitor jobs, content/design rules, allowed/excluded claims, uncertainty and approval state. Do not truncate deep business knowledge into a few slogans. Never pass private raw history or other tenants' data to a provider without exact authority.
2. **Research and visual observation.** Reuse public source retrieval/market research with a structured `ResearchFinding`: source URL/publisher/date, why relevant, observed market terminology/service convention, evidence strength, customer-specific implication and **separate rendered desktop/mobile visual evidence** when actually inspected. Compare **several independently operated peer websites** for Launch; refresh selectively for Ongoing. Treat absence of screenshot/visual inspection as unknown. External patterns are inspiration and market context, never proof of customer capability or permission to copy text, brand, images, CSS or distinctive layout.
3. **Structured intelligence / decision brief.** Reuse the existing Core workflow's Report/Direction/Blueprint capability, repaired so downstream creation actually sees source-bound conclusions. A small conceptual, case/version-bound `DecisionBrief` should include: user/buyer problem, target segment and intent, evidence references, alternatives, objections, proposed value, original creative strategy, proof gaps, approved claim sources, required/forbidden changes and why these choices matter. **A citation is not enough:** require inspectable evidence → inference → decision → outcome trace. Preserve proposed versus approved distinctions. Ongoing may make a narrow decision brief without recomputing all Launch artifacts.
4. **AI creative and component design.** Reuse common authoring/planning/visual intelligence for both modes. AI must be able to propose **new semantic Page/Section/block types** where justified by visitor needs, including content schema, data/source binding, responsive hierarchy, accessibility, image/interaction behavior and safe fallback. Do not limit intelligence to three existing Section roles, a larger static menu or the old accepted site's layout. Existing brand identity/design tokens and approved facts remain authoritative; previous site layouts are **nonbinding operator-accepted precedent**, not instructions to clone them. New blocks require reviewed, versioned, reusable implementations in the existing native WordPress compiler; **no unreviewed model-generated executable PHP/JS/CSS/HTML on a protected site**. An Article may use its existing bounded Article body model rather than the full site Section grammar.
5. **Proposal and delivery.** Reuse canonical Page/Section/Asset/Action identities, proposal/revision/preview/approval, native WordPress draft/stage/rollback contracts and source snapshots. Launch can plan a whole site; Ongoing targets an `ChangeSet` of existing canonical identities with `baseDigest`, changed fields/assets/routes, preservation obligations, reason, scope, reviewer decision and revert path. Never silently replace the entire site when improving one section; reconcile external WordPress edits and reject stale or foreign baselines.
6. **Observation and learning.** Use the existing first-party measurement/Performance Snapshot and Contact semantics, not a second analytics counter or fabricated event stream. Ongoing links an opportunity to a bounded `EvaluationPlan`: baseline period and coverage, primary meaningful signal, hypothesis, planned action, publication time, fair review window, known confounders and possible outcomes **working / promising / neutral / worse / inconclusive**. Unsupported causality or unobserved outcomes stay unknown. Learn for this customer first; cross-customer transfer remains gated, not assumed.

**Suggested conceptual records/interfaces** (names illustrative, map into existing Core/contracts instead of declaring tables now):
- `KnowledgeContext{scope,version,facts,approval,unknowns,brand,exclusions,sourceDigests}`
- `ResearchFinding{source,date,independence,method,textObservation,visualObservation?,limitation,decisionRelevance}`
- `DecisionBrief{objective,scope,baselineVersion,evidenceIds,inferences,choices,claimAuthority,uncertainties,creativeJobs}`
- `CreativeProposal{targetType,baseRevision?,page/section/article/asset/action/blockSpecs,reason,sourceTrace,visualPlan}`
- `ChangeEvaluation{opportunityId,proposalRevision,approvedRevision,actualApplyReceipt,baseline,metricDefinition,window,coverage,confounders,result,nextDecision}`

No single free-form prompt or catch-all `intelligence_json` blob should silently become the canonical truth. The existing **opportunity lifecycle** owns opportunity status and ranking; **content-growth** owns drafting/distribution; **customer journey** owns customer approval; **measurement** owns signal definitions. Link those authorities by IDs, do not clone them.

## Creation-specific orchestrator — broad, original, commercially credible

```text
Entry (Free Review OR Direct Start; progressive/resumable)
→ research current business + several actual comparable sites and contemporary visual practice
→ deep, original adaptive questions and confirmed customer knowledge
→ Business Understanding / Presence Direction / Conversion Architecture
→ reasoned IA, original Page/Section/Asset/Action plan, purpose-built new blocks if warranted
→ original commercial copy + safe native WordPress rendering
→ independent market/brand/UX critique, corrections and iteration
→ customer/Operator approvals → launch readiness and publication
→ first-party baseline and initial Ongoing opportunity inventory
```

At every AI step, downstream must receive relevant original, source/status-labeled synthesis; earlier failures included inadequate transfer of strategic context into Page generation. Prompts, task data projections, schemas, negative tests and renderer behavior must be evaluated together. Output and review must be **substantive**, not a low-cost receipt or working URL. **Preserve and improve valuable customer-owned R3 copy/design and other authorized work where useful.** Its historical layout is not mandatory. Third-party expression, branding and distinctive design have different rights: do not copy or closely paraphrase them. Keep source authority explicit; lawful owned-material reuse is not prohibited merely to force novelty. Do not make a fixed page count or number of canned questions the product ceiling.

## Ongoing-specific orchestrator — selective, change-aware and outcome-led

```text
Collect source-scoped activity/Contact and account/business-change signals
→ check coverage, baseline, customer capacity, known facts and existing opportunities
→ Maintain / Watch / Intervene, deduplicate and choose an explainable priority
→ optionally research fresh queries, peer changes or visual patterns if decision-relevant
→ choose: correct fact | improve existing Page/Action | original Article/content |
          add/retire content | new component/landing Page | fix supported delivery |
          improve measurement | ask a targeted business question | no change
→ source-bound hypothesis, expected value, required facts, scope/effort and measurement plan
→ targeted Core AI original draft or diff, native WordPress before/after preview
→ Operator QC and scoped customer approval (where required)
→ authorized apply with receipt + reversibility, no hidden broader site rewrite
→ measure at action-appropriate window; assess effect/uncertainty and update opportunity
→ feed relevant confirmed learning to customer knowledge/next decisions, then repeat
```

This is **not** the Launch report generator running again, nor a generic article schedule. The Ongoing AI may decide the best action is **leave the healthy Page alone**. It must consider accepted inquiry versus delivered notification versus qualified lead versus customer-confirmed outcome; a page view or CTA click is neither a lead nor a sale. Missing measurement is not zero. Existing Google data is optional, with its own coverage and identity.

**Examples of distinct Ongoing jobs (illustrative, not prewired branch logic):**
- *Poor-fit inquiries on an offer Page:* verify accepted Contacts and customer-confirmed fit, source coverage and capacity; research visitor expectations when useful; propose clearer qualification/process content and perhaps an original comparison block; review actual copy/Action changes, then monitor fit—not just total submissions.
- *New confirmed service or service area:* update canonical fact after approval; generate a change-impact list across affected Pages, Articles, Navigation, structured data and public claims; make reviewed diffs, preserve unrelated content and record publication/recheck.
- *Emerging search question with adequate relevant evidence:* research current terminology and competing treatment; propose a useful original Article or update an existing Page, linking to an actual Offering/visitor job; no keyword-filler or copied peer headings.
- *Low traffic, no live collector or sparse baseline:* report uncertainty, verify instrumentation or recommend **Watch**; never invent a statistical problem to trigger rewriting.

## AI/API policy — shared controls, differing tasks

**Shared model capabilities:** substantive synthesis; original market-facing writing; visual interpretation of actually rendered peer evidence; design and proposal of new component structures; planning, self-critique, targeted revisions and explanation. Reuse prompt modules and structured outputs where they serve the same task. **Do not make all stages use one small model, one generic instruction or a single flattened prompt** when reasoning/creative quality requires different model/tool settings. Test stage suitability against real output, not token cost alone.

**Creation request scope:** comprehensive evidence, strategic direction, complete conversion and page/section architecture, creative design briefs, whole-site coherence and first baseline. **Ongoing request scope:** versioned existing page/site snapshot, specific signal/change opportunity, prior approvals, relevant research delta, proposal target/diff, supported metric and preservation constraints. Both require evidence traces and a critical post-generation review, but Ongoing review must additionally check *unintended changes, baseline/version correctness and measurable business purpose*.

**Cost/privacy/authority:** choose useful calls under an **existing meaningful scope/budget grant**, metered conservatively; routine retries within grant should not require founder microapproval. An incremental Ongoing task should send **only relevant confirmed and permitted data**, not every customer record or raw lead messages. Separate model generation authority, Operator assessment, customer approval, protected WordPress write and public publication. No silent spend, privacy expansion, cross-tenant reuse, executable code publication or false proof.

## Concrete acceptance — two modes, one platform

**Shared data/intelligence:** Test different business knowledge or market findings cause **different** source-labeled, commercially justified choices and original WordPress output in both modes; inspect actual provider payload and output, not just source counts. Unknown/unapproved/private/other-tenant data cannot leak or turn into customer claims. Research several genuine independent peers for contemporary terminology and *rendered* visual design; past customer-accepted layout is a reference, never the only possible output. An original needed block specification must round-trip safely through WordPress without arbitrary executable code or regression.

**Creation:** A founder-acceptable, cohesive original C0 site that demonstrates deep knowledge/research, meaningful sections and media, complete valid Free Review and Direct Start visitor journeys, accurate staffed Launch→Ongoing and independent desktop/mobile critique. Neither technical tests nor a provider receipt constitute acceptance.

**Ongoing:** From a clearly labeled **synthetic or real** baseline: (a) select a supported opportunity or valid **Maintain/Watch**; (b) show evidence, uncertainty, meaningful target, reason and chosen outcome; (c) produce a genuinely AI-derived scoped original improvement, including a new component only if needed; (d) preserve unrelated Page/Article/Action identities/content and brand; (e) pass exact authority and preview/revision/rollback; (f) record a second observation under the same metric semantics; (g) show **working/promising/neutral/worse/inconclusive** honestly, never invent a business result. Customer and Operator can see what changed and why with targeted approval. Mechanical tests plus actual browser/content review and authority verification are all necessary.

**Cross-cutting negative tests:** Healthy page remains unchanged; short/sparse or broken measurement yields uncertainty not fabricated decline; unapproved customer fact is not published; raw Contact personal data never sent as article inspiration; outdated source or stale baseline is rejected; peer site content is neither reproduced nor treated as performance proof; synthetic metrics are never labeled real; a component cannot run uncontrolled model-generated code; incorrect tenant or unapproved WordPress release is refused; article-to-core promotion respects canonical ownership; previous customer corrections are not re-asked; original release/rollback can be restored. No unauthorized hosted write, credential change, public publish, merge or new provider grant.

**Operator / founder-facing success test:** The founder can review an original C0 site **and** a small, grounded Ongoing recommendation that explains a real issue (or transparently a synthetic exercise), proposes a specific creative change, previews it privately, and measures the right thing—**without being asked to design blocks or troubleshoot routine provider retries**.
